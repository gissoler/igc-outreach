Department of Health and Human Services. National Institutes of Health (NIH). National Institute on Aging (NIA).
Funding Opportunity Title: Data Coordinating Center for Alzheimer's Disease Research Data Harmonization (U24 Clinical Trial Not Allowed)
Funding Opportunity Number (FON): RFA-AG-27-010
Key Dates
Application Due Date: November 10, 2026
Purpose
This funding opportunity supports a data coordinating center that will harmonize multimodal Alzheimer's disease research datasets, develop open-source tools and maintain a community data portal.
Basic research projects that do not build shared data infrastructure will be returned without review.
Funding Instrument: Cooperative Agreement
Award Budget: Application budgets are limited to $2,500,000 in total costs per year.
Eligible Organizations
Nonprofits with 501(c)(3) IRS Status (Other than Institutions of Higher Education) are eligible to apply.
Public/State Controlled Institutions of Higher Education are eligible to apply.
Small Businesses are eligible to apply.
Non-domestic (non-U.S.) Entities (Foreign Institutions) are not eligible to apply.
Cost Sharing: This funding opportunity does not require cost sharing.
Applicants must integrate data from at least five AD-MAPs projects.
Eligibility clarifications are published in Notice NOT-AG-26-001 at https://grants.nih.gov/grants/guide/notice-files/NOT-AG-26-001.html
How to Apply: Applications must be submitted electronically through Grants.gov.
