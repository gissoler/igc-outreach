Provider of on-demand custom manufacturing platform for prototypes and production parts. The company connects buyers with a network of vetted suppliers offering instant quoting for CNC machining, 3D printing, sheet metal fabrication, injection molding, urethane casting, and metal stamping processes. Its platform supports rapid prototyping through high-volume production with no minimum orders, providing materials like metals, plastics, and composites along with finishing options and assembly services. Digital tools enable file upload, design analysis, project management, and supply chain traceability for startups to enterprises.
