## Overview
Johns Hopkins Technology Ventures (JHTV) is the technology commercialization arm of Johns Hopkins University, dedicated to translating the university's research excellence into accessible technologies, products, and services that benefit society. JHTV manages a diverse portfolio of over 130 commercial startups and has facilitated the licensing of more than 2,000 technologies across various disciplines. The organization offers a range of resources, including incubator spaces totaling 43,000 square feet, to support entrepreneurs in building sustainable, high-impact companies. Additionally, JHTV has been instrumental in raising over $4.8 billion in venture funding for its portfolio companies, underscoring its significant role in fostering innovation and economic development. Strategically, JHTV focuses on sectors such as technology, healthcare, biotechnology, medical devices, diagnostics, digital health, information technology, engineering, environmental services, consumer products, pharmaceuticals, energy, materials science, aerospace, and defense. The organization supports companies at various stages of development, from seed and early-stage ventures to growth, late-stage, and pre-IPO companies. This comprehensive approach enables JHTV to address a wide array of industries and investment opportunities, aligning with its mission to maximize the impact of Johns Hopkins University's research. Through its initiatives, JHTV has established numerous active research collaborations and corporate partnerships, facilitating the translation of novel scientific and industrial discoveries into real-world applications. The organization regularly hosts investor visits to campus and connects entrepreneurs with potential funders, fostering a vibrant ecosystem that bridges academic research and commercial enterprise. By leveraging the strengths of academia, industry, and government, JHTV continues to drive innovation and contribute to societal advancement.
