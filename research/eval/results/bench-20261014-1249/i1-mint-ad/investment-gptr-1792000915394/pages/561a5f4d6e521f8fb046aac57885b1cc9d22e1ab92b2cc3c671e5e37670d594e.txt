# Unlearn Partners with Acumen Pharmaceuticals to Advance Alzheimer’s Disease Clinical Trials Using AI Digital Twins

175
SAN FRANCISCO, CA AND NEWTON, MA — July 9, 2026 — Leads & Copy — Unlearn, a company specializing in AI-powered solutions for clinical development, has announced a collaboration with Acumen Pharmaceuticals, a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company focused on novel therapeutics for Alzheimer’s disease. The partnership aims to leverage Unlearn’s AI-generated digital twins of trial participants within two of Acumen’s ongoing Alzheimer’s disease clinical trials for their antibody, sabirnetug.

Digital twins are designed to provide comprehensive forecasts of individual participant outcomes across future timepoints in a study. This technology is intended to enhance insights derived from existing trial data and support more informed decision-making throughout the clinical development process.

Steve Herne, CEO of Unlearn, expressed enthusiasm for the collaboration, stating, “Alzheimer’s is a highly challenging disease in drug development, and the teams working on it deserve better analytical tools.” He added, “We look forward to working with the Acumen team to apply our tools and expertise across the company’s clinical portfolio. Alzheimer’s disease is an area where we see a strong fit for digital twins to potentially expedite development of new therapies.”

Unlearn’s digital twins are generated using a specialized Digital Twin Generator for Alzheimer’s disease, which has been trained on over 26,000 patient records from historical clinical data. The company’s method, which is EMA-qualified and FDA-supported, integrates these individualized digital twins into trial analyses. This approach is intended to provide a more precise comparison for evaluating treatment effects.

Jim Doherty, President and Chief Development Officer of Acumen, commented on the partnership: “We are excited to work with the Unlearn team to apply its digital twin technology to generate deeper insights from our clinical studies.” He continued, “As we advance sabirnetug through clinical development, maximizing insights from every study is critical. This collaboration with Unlearn aligns with our commitment to leveraging innovative, scientifically rigorous tools that can strengthen evidence as we work toward potential Phase 3 planning.”

Unlearn aims to transform clinical development by making trials more efficient and data-driven through the use of AI and digital twins. The company works with pharmaceutical and biotechnology firms to facilitate faster, more robust studies and clearer decision-making. Unlearn emphasizes its science-first approach and regulatory engagement, including EMA qualification and FDA support.

Acumen Pharmaceuticals is developing a therapeutic targeting toxic soluble amyloid beta oligomers (AβOs) for Alzheimer’s disease. The company’s lead candidate, sabirnetug (ACU193), is a humanized monoclonal antibody. Acumen is currently conducting the Phase 2 trial ALTITUDE-AD, evaluating sabirnetug in early Alzheimer’s disease. The trial has enrolled 542 participants across multiple international sites, with topline results anticipated in late 2026. Acumen is also exploring a subcutaneous formulation of sabirnetug using Halozyme’s ENHANZE® technology and collaborating with JCR Pharmaceuticals on an Enhanced Brain Delivery (EBD™) therapy.
