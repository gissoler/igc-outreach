## IGC Pharma, Inc. (IGC) - Q1 2026 10-Q Summary

### Business Context and Reporting Period

This summary covers the quarterly period ended March 31, 2026 . IGC Pharma, Inc. is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing therapeutic candidates for neuropsychiatric and neurodegenerative disorders, primarily Alzheimer's disease. The company's lead product, IGC-AD1 , is currently in a Phase 2 clinical trial (CALMA) for agitation in Alzheimer's dementia. The company also develops the MINT-AD AI platform for risk stratification. IGC operates as a smaller reporting company and is not yet profitable, relying on equity and debt financing to fund operations.

### Material Changes vs. Prior Period

Revenue Decline: Revenue decreased 4% to $317,000, driven by a strategic shift away from white-label services to focus on clinical trials. Gross margin compressed significantly to 17% from 47% due to higher cost of revenue relative to sales volume.

Increased Operating Loss: Net loss more than doubled to $2.4 million. This was driven by a 118% increase in SG&A expenses (partially due to the absence of a $700k credit recognized in the prior year) and a 28% increase in R&D expenses as the CALMA trial progressed.

Liquidity Deterioration: Cash and cash equivalents dropped 77% to $207,000. The working capital deficit widened to $1.7 million from $366,000.

Debt Expansion: Total debt increased significantly to $917,000 from $311,000. This includes new convertible notes from Vanquish Funding Group ($353k principal) and an OnDeck loan ($214k), alongside related-party borrowings.

### Guidance, Outlook, and Risks

Outlook and Strategy: Management prioritizes completing the Phase 2 CALMA trial for IGC-AD1 and deploying the MINT-AD and AHA AI platforms. The company expects to continue incurring operating losses. Management believes current cash, combined with a $12 million credit facility (currently undrawn) and ongoing financing efforts, is sufficient to support operations for at least the next 12 months.

Recent Financing Activity:

Issued a $353k convertible note to Vanquish Funding Group (VFG) in March 2026.

Secured a $214k loan from OnDeck Capital in February 2026.

Completed a registered direct offering in January 2026 raising ~$234k.

Subsequent to quarter-end (April 2026), the company issued two additional convertible notes totaling ~$585k principal to VFG and FirstFire Global Opportunities Fund.

Risks and Contingencies:

Clinical Risk: Success is highly correlated with the outcome of the CALMA trial. Delays or negative results could materially adversely affect the business.

Liquidity Risk: The company requires substantial additional capital. Failure to secure financing could force delays or discontinuation of programs.

Dilution Risk: Convertible notes contain variable-rate conversion features (e.g., 75% of lowest trading price upon default), which could result in substantial dilution to existing shareholders.

Regulatory Risk: Uncertainty regarding FDA approval for hemp-based products and AI diagnostic tools.
