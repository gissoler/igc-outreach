# Biomodal — Research Notes

## Overview

Biomodal (formerly Cambridge Epigenetix) is a Cambridge, UK-based life sciences tools and analytics company founded in 2012 by Professor Sir Shankar Balasubramanian and Bobby Yerramilli-Rao. It rebranded from Cambridge Epigenetix to biomodal in April 2023, alongside the commercial launch of its first duet multiomics product. The company develops duet multiomics technology for simultaneous sequencing of genetic and epigenetic information, including the "6-base genome" (A, T, G, C, 5mC, and 5hmC), using existing sequencing infrastructure. [1] [2]

## Technology

duet Multiomics Platform : Simultaneous sequencing of all six DNA bases — four standard bases plus two modified cytosines (5-methylcytosine/5mC and 5-hydroxymethylcytosine/5hmC). Compatible with all major sequencing platforms. Enables combined genetic and epigenetic analysis from a single sequencing run. [3]

Applications : Oncology (cancer epigenomics), neuroscience, rare disease, clinical diagnostics. The 5hmC modification is particularly informative for cancer and neurological disease. [1]

Certified Service Provider Program : Launched 2024 to expand access to duet 5-base and 6-base sequencing through partner labs. [4]

## Funding & Investors

Biomodal's latest clearly disclosed financing found in public sources is the November 2021 $88 million Series D, led by Temasek with participation from new investor Third Point and existing investors GV, New Science Ventures, Ahren Innovation Capital, and Sequoia. The company's own Series D announcement said the round brought total funds raised to date to $146 million; third-party data providers report totals around $145 million to $164 million, but no later named financing round was found in public web research. The 2021 proceeds were intended to commercialize Biomodal's genetic and epigenetic sequencing platform, which integrates with existing sequencing platforms rather than replacing them. The company's current "About" timeline says Cambridge Epigenetix received $98 million in funding in 2020 to develop duet multiomics technology, but the public round announcements support a $88 million Series D announced in November 2021 and $146 million cumulative funding after that round; the $98 million timeline entry was not treated as a separate financing event without another supporting source. [5] [18]

## Investors

Current and historical investors cited across company and investor materials include Temasek, Third Point, GV, New Science Ventures, Ahren Innovation Capital, Sequoia Capital, Syncona Partners, Cambridge Enterprise, Cambridge University, and JIMCO Life Sciences Fund. JIMCO states that its Life Sciences Fund participated in Biomodal's US$88 million Series D round in late 2021. Sequoia lists Biomodal as a portfolio company and describes the company as an omics-based life-sciences technology and analytics business. [5] [19] [20]
