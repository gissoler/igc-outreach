For medical device startups, funding is never far from mind. A device doesn’t make the leap from a design on paper to a patient’s bedside without the right financial backing.

And while venture capital is not the only way to fund your business (bootstrapping and family offices may also be options) it is one of the most popular and well-known routes that startups can take.

That’s why we’ve put together this list. Here, you’ll find some of the most reputable and successful venture capital funds that either focus specifically on medtech and life sciences, or emphasize medical devices in their portfolios. The list is heavily weighted toward the US, but many of the funds have a presence in Europe and/or Asia, as well.

Just remember, this list is a resource, not a ranking. We encourage you to explore the list fully, as there are many different types of funds represented with different portfolios, investment styles, and preferences on company growth stage.

### OrbiMed

A leading healthcare-focused investment firm with a portfolio spanning biopharmaceuticals, medical devices, diagnostics, and healthcare IT. OrbiMed has offices in New York City, San Francisco, London, Shanghai, and Mumbai.

https://www.orbimed.com/

### New Enterprise Associates (NEA)

A global venture capital firm with a long-standing and significant commitment to healthcare and medical technology. NEA was founded in 1977 and is headquartered in Chevy Chase, Maryland, with offices in Menlo Park, California, and Beijing.

https://www.nea.com/

### Versant Ventures

A leading investor in the biotechnology, medical device, and healthcare spaces, known for helping entrepreneurs with groundbreaking ideas. Versant Ventures was founded in 1999 and has offices in the US, Canada, and Europe.

https://www.versantventures.com/

### SV Health Investors

A healthcare and life sciences venture capital firm that invests across a range of opportunities, from early-stage to growth equity. SV Health Investors was founded in 1993 and is located in Boston and London.

https://www.svhealthinvestors.com/

### Solas BioVentures

A venture capital firm that invests exclusively in the life sciences industry, focusing on early-stage biopharmaceuticals, medical devices, digital health, and diagnostics. The firm was founded in 2015 and is headquartered in Chattanooga, Tennessee.

https://www.solasbio.com/

### SOSV

A global deep tech venture capital firm that operates the HAX and IndieBio startup programs, both with a strong focus on human and planetary health, including medical devices. The firm has offices in the US, Europe, and Asia, and was founded in 1995.

https://sosv.com/

### General Catalyst

A firm with a significant presence in digital health through its Health Assurance Fund, which backs companies transforming the healthcare system. The firm was founded in 2000 in Massachusetts and has offices in Boston, Palo Alto, San Francisco, and New York.

https://www.generalcatalyst.com/

### Third Rock Ventures

Known for its hands-on approach, this firm creates and invests in life science companies from the ground up, including those in the medical device sector. Third Rock Ventures was founded in 2007 and has offices in Boston and San Francisco.

https://www.thirdrockventures.com/

### 7wireVentures

An early-stage venture capital firm focused on companies that are building the digital health ecosystem to empower consumers and improve care. The firm was founded in 2011 and is based in Chicago, Illinois.

https://www.7wireventures.com/

### ARCH Venture Partners

A top-tier firm that provides seed/early-stage funding to companies in the life sciences, physical sciences, and information technology. ARCH was founded in 1986 and has offices in Chicago, San Francisco, and Seattle.

https://www.archventure.com/

### Vensana Capital

A firm exclusively dedicated to partnering with entrepreneurs in the medical technology sector. Vensana Capital was founded in 2019 and is located in the Bay Area, California.

https://www.vensanacap.com/

### Arboretum Ventures

A Midwest-based healthcare venture capital firm that invests in innovations that solve significant healthcare problems. Arboretum Ventures was founded in 2002 and is located in Ann Arbor, Michigan.

https://www.arboretumvc.com/

### BioVentures MedTech Funds

A venture capital firm exclusively focused on investing in companies that are developing leading-edge medical technologies. The firm was founded in 1999 and is located in Vienna, Virginia.

https://www.bioventuresinvestors.com/

### Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)

While a well-known general tech firm, a16z has become a major force in health tech through its Bio + Health Fund, which invests in companies at the intersection of biology, health, and technology. The firm was founded in 2009 in Silicon Valley, California.

https://a16z.com/bio-health/

### Sofinnova Partners

A leading European venture capital firm specializing in life sciences, with a strong focus on healthcare and sustainability. Sofinnova was founded in 1972 and has offices in Paris, London, and Milan.

https://www.sofinnovapartners.com/

### 5AM Ventures

This firm focuses on early-stage life science companies, including those in the medical device space. 5AM Ventures was founded in 2002 and is located in California.

https://5amventures.com/

### Venrock

A venture capital firm with a strong history of investing in the diagnostics, medical device, and therapeutics sectors. Venrock was founded in 1969 as the venture capital arm of the Rockefeller family and is located in Palo Alto, California, and Cambridge, Massachusetts.

https://www.venrock.com/

### Sante Ventures

A specialized firm that invests in biotech, medtech, and health tech companies. Sante Ventures was founded in 2006 and is located in Austin, Texas.

https://sante.com/

### Longitude Capital

A healthcare-focused investment firm that funds both private and public companies. The firm was founded in 2006 and is headquartered in Menlo Park, California, with an additional office in Greenwich, Connecticut.

https://www.longitudecapital.com/

### Domain Associates LLC

A venture capital firm that focuses on biopharma, device, and diagnostic companies. Domain Associates was founded in 1985 and is based in Princeton, New Jersey.

https://domainvc.com/

### Vivo Capital

A healthcare-focused investment firm that invests in companies across all stages of development. Vivo Capital was founded in 1997 and has offices in Palo Alto, California, and Shanghai, China.

https://www.vivocapital.com/

### Innova Memphis

A pre-seed, seed, and early-stage investor with a strong record of funding high-growth medical device startups in the U.S. The firm was founded in 2007 and is located in Memphis, Tennessee.

https://www.innovamemphis.com/

### Catalyst Health Ventures (CHV)

An early-stage venture capital firm focused on the medical device, diagnostic, and digital health sectors. The firm was founded in 1998 and is based in Braintree, Massachusetts.

https://chv.vc/

### Lightspeed Venture Partners

While a multi-industry firm, Lightspeed has been expanding its healthcare investments, with an emphasis on tech-enabled medical solutions. Lightspeed was founded in 2000 and is headquartered in Menlo Park, California.

https://lsvp.com/

### Kleiner Perkins

A prominent venture capital firm with a history of investing in life sciences, including MedTech. Kleiner Perkins was founded in 1972 and is located in Menlo Park, California.

https://www.kleinerperkins.com/

https://sondercapital.com/

The corporate venture arm of Medtronic, focusing on startups that align with their strategic interests in medical devices. Medtronic Ventures was founded in 2004 and is headquartered in Minneapolis, Minnesota.

https://www.medtronic.com/us-en/about/ventures.html

### Deerfield Management

A healthcare investment firm that works to advance patient care through a range of investment strategies, including venture capital. Deerfield Management was founded in 1994 and is based in New York City.

https://deerfield.com/

### Texas Medical Center Venture Fund

### Cultivate(MD)

A fund specifically focused on medical device venture capital, often investing at an early stage. The firm was founded in 2014 and is based in Grand Rapids, Michigan.

https://cultivate-md.com/

A venture firm that supports companies in cardiovascular and cerebrovascular innovation. Broadview Ventures was founded in 2008 and is headquartered in Boston, Massachusetts.

### Health Catalyst

### Gilde Healthcare

https://gildehealthcare.com/

### Baird Capital

A global private equity and venture capital firm that invests in the healthcare sector, including medical devices. Baird Capital was founded in 1989 and is headquartered in Chicago, Illinois.

### The Vertical Group

A firm with a long history of investing exclusively in the medical device sector. The Vertical Group was founded in 1989 and is headquartered in Summit, New Jersey.

A venture debt firm that provides capital to life sciences and healthcare companies, including those in the medical device space. Oxford Finance was founded in 1998 and is headquartered in Alexandria, Virginia.

### J&J Development Corp. (JJDC)

The venture capital subsidiary of Johnson & Johnson, actively investing in healthcare and medical device startups. JJDC was founded in 1973 and has a global presence.

A venture capital firm with a focus on life science, including medical devices, biopharmaceuticals, and diagnostics. Hatteras Venture Partners was founded in 2000 and is based in Durham, North Carolina.

### Canaan Partners

A venture capital firm that invests in early-stage technology and healthcare companies. Canaan Partners was founded in 1987 and is headquartered in Menlo Park, California, with offices in New York City and Tel Aviv.

### Schooner Capital
