### 4 . N-of-One

NGS analysis, interpretation, and reporting solutions for research and clinical applications. They provide end-to-end solutions for Next Generation Sequencing (NGS) data, catering to both research and clinical needs. Their offerings span secondary analysis, interpretation, and visualization, leveraging knowledge bases and pathway analysis. They offer cloud-based solutions, clinical decision support software, and professional clinical interpretation services. They support various applications including biomedical genomics, microbial genomics, single-cell sequencing, and translational research. Their services aid in biomarker identification, target discovery, and drug discovery. They offer solutions for oncology, hereditary diseases, and rare & undiagnosed diseases. They provide tools for variant analysis, data and pipeline management, and access to curated genomic data. They offer clinical testing solutions, knowledge bases, and services to improve test turnaround times and clinical reporting capabilities. They provide expert-curated content and professional interpretation services for molecular oncology labs. They offer software, databases, and services for researchers and clinicians in the fields of genomics and precision medicine.
