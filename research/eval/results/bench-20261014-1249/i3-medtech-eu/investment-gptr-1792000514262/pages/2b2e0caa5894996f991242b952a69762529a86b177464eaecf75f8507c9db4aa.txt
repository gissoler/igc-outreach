## ABOUT THE European Medical Device Summit
The European Medical Device Summit is a leading medtech event in Europe, offering actionable strategies to advance medical device development, design, product development technologies, and regulatory affairs.
Join over 200 fellow medical device leaders to explore advanced medical device technologies such as eQMS, continued process verification (CPV), and regulatory reporting. Hear from some of the largest medical technology companies on QMS management, ISO standards for medical devices, and change management in digital transformation.
Gain insights to streamline medtech design and sustainability, mitigate risks, reduce costs, accelerate time-to-market, and ensure compliance with European medical device regulations, such as EU MDR. Discover advancements in AI in medical devices, IoMT, product development, and medical technologies while tackling challenges in manufacturing medical devices, digital transformation, and managing clinical trials.

## Key Themes
PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT Accelerating product development through AI-driven predictive modeling and automated requirements management workflows
Integrating rapid prototyping, micro-molding, and advanced 3D printing for faster feasibility iterations
Leveraging real-world data and evidence to guide earlier product design and market decisions
Strengthening cross-functional project management using modern Enterprise and Portfolio Management solutions
Enhancing development efficiency through design for manufacturability and lean engineering processes
Deploying cloud-based product lifecycle management platforms enabling seamless global collaboration
Optimizing supplier visibility and traceability through connected digital supply chain technologies
Incorporating neurotech and emerging sensor technologies to support next-generation medical device platforms
Applying advanced material testing and extractables/leachables analysis earlier in the development cycle
Driving operational excellence with workflow automation, business process automation, and integrated eQMS systems

DESIGN & INNOVATION Advancing human-centric design informed by behavioral insights and rigorous human factors engineering
Expanding connected device capabilities using IoT architectures and user-friendly digital interfaces
Employing UX/UI best practices to improve clinical usability, patient engagement, and product adoption
Integrating disruptive innovation strategies to accelerate breakthrough concepts and technology scouting
Using AI-powered electronics design and simulation tools to optimize complex device architectures
Implementing design verification enhancements leveraging virtual prototyping and software-in-the-loop testing
Innovating surface treatments, coatings, and material technologies for performance and durability improvements
Strengthening design for manufacturability approaches across plastics molding, thermoforming, and tooling operations
Enabling rapid manufacturing with agile engineering teams and outsourced product development partnerships
Embedding cybersecurity considerations directly into early device design and digital feature planning

QUALITY & REGULATORY Navigating evolving EU MDR and global regulatory frameworks with centralized RIM and RI tools
Enhancing post-market surveillance using real-time analytics, RWE/RWD, and automated safety reporting capabilities
Strengthening risk management integration across design, manufacturing, and lifecycle quality programs
Modernizing QMS with scalable eQMS platforms supporting validation, document control, and eDHR solutions
Improving regulatory writing quality through structured content management and specialized medical writing support
Addressing Software as a Medical Device requirements with robust testing and lifecycle documentation strategies
Ensuring cybersecurity compliance through continuous monitoring, threat modeling, and regulatory-aligned practices
Optimizing process validation, sterilization validation, and packaging validation under ISO 13485 expectations
Advancing international market penetration strategies through coordinated global regulatory intelligence efforts
Strengthening audit readiness with automated data trails, supplier quality oversight, and compliance traceability

## Frequently Asked Questions
Who attends this summit as delegates? At the European Medical Device Summit, our delegates are C-Suite Executives, VPs, Directors, and other leaders at mid to large-sized medical device companies. We have strong delegate representation in manufacturing, supply chain, operations, product development, quality & regulatory, engineering & technology. To view our full delegate list, please visit emdsummit.com/delegates

Who attends this summit as sponsors? Each year, the sponsor line-up at the European Medical Device Summit is composed of top solution providers who specialize in artificial intelligence, QMS, cybersecurity, PLM, regulatory intelligence, contract manufacturing, labelling, prototyping, MDR, and more. Check out our sponsor line-up at emdsummit.com/sponsors

How many delegates do we expect to attend the European Medical Device Summit? We look forward to welcoming about 110 medical device leaders at this year’s summit.

How many sponsors will be at the European Medical Device Summit? Approximately 45 sponsors will be present at this summit, providing our delegates with the opportunity to evaluate new solutions and emerging technologies.

What networking opportunities are available? At the European Medical Device Summit, there are countless networking opportunities during our drink receptions, breakfast sessions, lunch & learn roundtables, dedicated networking breaks for pre-arranged 1-2-1 meetings, and more. For pre-arranged meetings, we curate 1-2-1s between top solution providers and industry executives based on their key investment areas.
