## Are You a HealthTech Buyer? Join the MedTech World Buyer Connect Program

The MedTech World Buyer Connect Program is designed for qualified buyers seeking meaningful connections with solution providers, innovators, and startups. Approved buyers receive a complimentary Platinum Pass to the relevant MedTech World edition, access to the dedicated Buyer Lounge and matching platform, priority matchmaking with sponsors and exhibitors aligned to their sourcing interests, and invitations to selected networking sessions throughout the event.

In return, participants are expected to attend a minimum of eight curated one-to-one meetings , ensuring valuable and relevant business conversations while making the most of their time onsite. Please note that complimentary passes are issued at the Standard tier only and do not include access to premium ticket categories. Buyers who do not fulfil the minimum meeting commitment will be invoiced at the Standard ticket rate. Retroactive discounts, refunds, or adjustments to existing bookings are not applicable.

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis, and our team may request additional details to complete your approval.

##
FAQs

###
Who should attend MedTech World?

MedTech World brings together the entire healthcare innovation ecosystem. It’s the ideal event for startup founders, investors, healthcare leaders, government representatives, service providers, and corporates who want to connect, collaborate, and explore the future of MedTech. Whether you’re building a company, funding innovation, shaping policy, or seeking partnerships, MedTech World offers a global stage to meet the right people, discover cutting-edge technologies, and accelerate growth.

###
How do I register for the event?

Registration is available through our official website . Simply select your ticket type, fill in the required details, and complete payment online.

###
Is there a networking and matchmaking platform?

Yes. MedTech World provides a dedicated networking and meeting platform designed to facilitate meaningful connections between investors, founders, corporates, and ecosystem leaders. The platform opens 4 weeks before the conference, allowing attendees to browse profiles, request meetings, and schedule 1:1 sessions in advance. It remains active for 14 days after the event to support continued follow ups and deal discussions. For Investor Pass holders, introductions are both platform enabled and selectively curated by our team to ensure high quality, relevant deal flow.

###
Are there any Exhibition, Presenting or sponsorship opportunities?

Yes — MedTech World offers a full suite of opportunities tailored to elevate your brand, engage key stakeholders, and showcase your innovations. Contact our team to find the right option for your brand.

###
Will the sessions be available after the event?

Yes. All sessions will be made available after the event across our social media platforms and on our official YouTube channel .

###
Can I download the 2026 MedTech World Ecosystem Report?

Yes. Our 2026 Ecosystem Report is available for download and provides an overview of the investors, startups, corporates, service providers, and institutional partners that make up the MedTech World community. The report offers valuable insights into the profile, geographic reach, and sector focus of our global ecosystem. Simply complete the short form to access your copy.

###
What kind of networking opportunities are available during any of MedTech World events?

MedTech World offers multiple structured networking opportunities throughout the conference. These include a dedicated Investors Lounge located on the exhibition floor, curated investor and leadership luncheons, private dinners, sponsored networking breakfasts, and the MedTech World Gala Awards Ceremony. All networking moments are officially scheduled and facilitated by our team to ensure meaningful, high value interactions rather than purely informal meetups.
