## General information

Short Summary
A Spanish clinical-stage MedTech SME has developed a patented non-invasive cardiac monitor with a 30-second multisensor device (ECG,acoustic PCG,PPG) and a cloud AI engine. Already with European Patent granted, manufacturing license, and CE Class IIa in progress, collaboration is sought for:

1)B2B commercial distributors and e-health integrators for hospital expansion.

2)Venture investors for the open 800k€ final tranche (€50k tickets)

Commercial, supplier or investment agreements are offered. Full Description
Cardiovascular diseases remain the leading cause of global mortality. Existing outpatient solutions (such as standard Holter ECGs) capture electrical cardiac activity over 24–48 hours but fail to evaluate mechanical heart performance, heart sounds, or extract hemodynamic KPIs in real time, leading to delayed diagnoses of heart failure and frequent hospital readmissions.

To solve this critical clinical gap, a Spanish MedTech company has engineered a proprietary, non-invasive point-of-care cardiac monitor. The portable device captures synchronised tri-modal signals—electrocardiography (ECG), phonocardiography (PCG / cardiac acoustics), and photoplethysmography (PPG / pulse wave)—in a single 30-second measurement from the patient's home or doctor's office.

The company new device improves the quality of life of heart failure patients without requiring them to go to the hospital, since its evolution will be monitored systematically and automatically because it is a non-invasive sensor. It is an aid to cardiologists' decisions, thus improving patient care. Considering a problem-solution frame, the device innovation technology helps to:

1.Prediction of NTproBNP Biomarker: Provides predictions with a 15-day lead time.

2. Prediction and Identification of Heart Failure Events: Detects signs such as Spider Angiomas (indicating dilated blood vessels), circulation issues, Cyanosis (sign of reduced blood flow), and Xanthelasma on eyelids (cholesterol buildup, increased HF risk), with a 15-day lead time.

3.Reduction in Diagnostic Time: the device is not a replacement but a support tool for healthcare professionals. It provides critical information to aid their work, allows semi-passive health monitoring for users, significantly reduces costs associated with managing heart failure patients, and facilitates early detection.

4.Improved Quality of Life: Enhances life quality for people, especially in demographic challenge areas and aging populations with limited access to healthcare centers.

5.Reduction of Gender Health Disparities: The generated data will allow for specific pre-diagnoses based on individual histories, normalizing healthcare through teletriage.

6.Efficient Healthcare Planning**: Supports more effective healthcare planning, considering territorial, generational, and gender aspects.

7.Short Learning Curve: Focuses on ease of access to information.

8.Reduction of Hospital Costs: Decreases hospital expenses related to heart failure admissions in implementing centers.

9.Decreased Patient Expenses: Reduces out-of-pocket costs for patients in regions without public healthcare systems.

The company is actively:

Structuring its international commercial network to deploy its platform across primary care, cardiology units, internal medicine units, emergency departments, nursing homes, home-telehealth providers, insurance companies, and clinical trial research organisations (CROs).

Completing a late-stage Seed / Pre-Series A equity financing round of € 2M. The majority of the round is already committed, including strategic sector investors — a physician-led investor group and medical device distribution partners — alongside other backers, and the firm is opening a final co-investment tranche of up to €800,000 to complete the syndicate. Investment is offered under standard MedTech venture valuation multiples, reflecting a clinical-stage asset with granted European IP, TRL 7-8 maturity, multi-center hospital validation data, externally audited financial accounts, and imminent Class IIa CE Mark certification.

Cooperation is sought under commercial, supplier or investment agreements. Expertise sought and roles for each cooperation are described below. Advantages and Innovations
Regarding advantages and innovation, this can be summed up regarding an integrated business maturity readiness approach highligting referred milestones already accomplished:

--Core Technological Capabilities:

Electro-Mechanical Biomarker Extraction: Advanced cloud-based AI algorithms measure electro-mechanical parameters, including electromechanical delay (EMD) and heart sounds and pulse, deriving hemodynamic parameters from them, detecting early subclinical heart failure, with valvular anomaly detection planned as a future capability

--Clinical validation status:

Proven Clinical Validation: Tested in multi-center clinical trials across 8 major hospitals involving >100 patients, demonstrating high sensitivity and specificity compared to gold-standard clinical parameters measured in hospital settings, such as NT-proBNP, B-lines, and inferior vena cava diameter.

--IP and regulatory status:

Granted European Patent (EP) securing the sensor fusion hardware architecture, complemented by proprietary algorithms protected as industrial/trade secret know-how; Manufacturing License granted by the Spanish Agency of Medicines and Medical Devices (AEMPS), CE Mark Class IIa certification in final review, with the notified body's decision expected by Q4 2026 and, pending a closing on-site audit, certification anticipated by Q4 2026; TRL 7-8.

--Financial transparency and investor base.

Annual accounts externally audited on a voluntary basis, an uncommon standard of financial rigor at this stage. The round has attracted strategic, sector-specialist investors rather than purely financial funds, reinforcing market validation. Considering the use of Proceeds: Funds will be deployed directly into completing final CE Mark regulatory clearance, FDA pre-submission preparation, expanding production, and executing initial commercial pilots with distribution partners in Europe and North America.
