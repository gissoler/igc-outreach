## Angels4Health

A passionate group of more than 30 seasoned angel investors dedicated to driving innovation and growth in life science and healthcare.

Regular virtual pitch events on the first Thursday of each month.

### We invest in Life Science / Healthcare Ventures

Angels4Health is a network of private angel investors focused on early stage ventures in the life science industry, including:

Therapeutics/Pharma/Biotech/Supplements

Diagnostics/Analytics/Companion Diagnostics

Digital Health/AI/Digital Health Apps (DIGA)

MedTech

### We are more than just investors — we are partners

We commonly share the objective of being more than just financial investors, but of being mentors, sparring partners and part of the team. We want to support early stage ventures on their way to huge funding rounds and exits.

### More than checks — what to expect from us

We are former entrepreneurs, physicians or senior business leaders from small, medium and large sized Life Sciences/ Healthcare companies. When investing, we provide capital, expertise and network to help entrepreneurs turn great ideas into great companies and we always bring in all three of them when working together with startups.
