## The challenge

Healthcare systems are facing increasing pressure from a shortage of skilled professionals, rising patient volumes, and the need to improve efficiency and consistency of care.

To help address these challenges, Siemens Healthineers is seeking innovative startup solutions that enable higher levels of autonomy in:

medical imaging devices

surgical robotics

interventional oncology systems (e.g. radiotherapy, ablation)

corresponding clinical workflows

Siemens Healthineers is particularly interested in technologies that leverage artificial intelligence, advanced sensing, computer vision, robotics, intelligent automation to support autonomous operation decision-making, workflow orchestration, and intervention execution.

Solutions may range from software-based intelligence to robotic systems that reduce manual effort, increase productivity, and help healthcare providers deliver high-quality care with limited resources.

The challenge is ideal for companies with solutions that are ready for testing by Siemens Healthineers, meaning a technological readiness level of 7 or above.
