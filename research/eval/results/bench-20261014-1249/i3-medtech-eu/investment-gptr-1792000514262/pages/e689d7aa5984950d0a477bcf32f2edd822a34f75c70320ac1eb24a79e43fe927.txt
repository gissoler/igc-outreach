## How Spain Investors Differ from US Coastal Capital

Three patterns matter when you cold-outreach Spain angels vs reaching out to NYC or SF:

Check size is typically EUR 25-100k for an angel and EUR 200-500k for an early-stage fund (compare to USD 50-250k angel / USD 500k-2M seed lead in the US). Plan your round structure accordingly - more angels at smaller checks rather than a single lead.

Speed is usually slower at the first-meeting stage but faster at the term-sheet stage. Spain investors tend to do more due-diligence calls before committing, but once they commit they sign within 1-2 weeks.

Sector concentration in Spain skews toward SaaS, fintech, climate tech, and deep tech. Pure consumer apps and US-style growth plays get less traction.
