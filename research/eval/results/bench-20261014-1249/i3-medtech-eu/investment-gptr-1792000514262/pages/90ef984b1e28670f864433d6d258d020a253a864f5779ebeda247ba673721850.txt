# IDOVEN

https://idoven.ai/ 
Last activity: 05.10.2026

Active





Categories: Analytics Artificial Intelligence Care Development Hardware HealthTech MedTech Platform Research Technology


IDOVEN is a European HealthTech startup company based in Madrid that seeks to prevent cardiac diseases, myocardial infarction and sudden death. To do this, we are redefining the way cardiac arrhythmias are diagnosed by deploying technology based on artificial intelligence algorithms and digital health solutions.

IDOVEN offers top-quality cardiologic reports based on cutting edge technology, which improves diagnosis precision supporting a more accurate clinical decision. The aim of IDOVEN is to transform clinical practice into a faster, more precise, and more preventive way of solving heart disorders.

We have received the Seal of Excellence from the European Commission.

We are Google for Startups Resident at Google Campus Madrid and IDOVEN is supported by EIT Health (EIT Health is a network of best-in-class health innovators backed by the European Union).
