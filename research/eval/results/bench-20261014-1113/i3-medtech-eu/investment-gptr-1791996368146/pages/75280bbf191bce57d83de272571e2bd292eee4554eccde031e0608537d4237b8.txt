This sector includes wearable technology (also called wearable gadgets) is a category of technology devices that can be worn by individuals or pets.
Wearable Technology sector is also known as Smart Wearables.
Companies which manufacture wearable devices for fitness tracking like bands, wellness devices for sleep monitoring, sexual wellness, posture correction. Body wears like shirts, pants, shoes etc which have the ability to track matrices like movements, pressure, calories burned etc. Healthcare devices which can be worn and help in chronic disease management, heart care, diabetes etc. Companies making wearables for tracking and monitoring babies, elderly, pets and patients. This also includes wearable devices for infotainment purposes (Smart watches, communication devices, wireless earphones, wearable camera). High-end fashion wearables like jewellery, watch, bracelet etc with features like fitness monitoring, notification alerts etc. Enterprise wearable devices for safety, wellness and increasing the efficiency of employees. ### 1 . Devicare

Devicare develops portable, home solutions for diagnosis, prognosis, treatment, and monitoring of chronic diseases. It distributes its products through agreements with pharmaceutical companies or In Vitro Diagnostic IVD companies. These POC medical devices are useful in measuring the level of biomarkers in body fluids such as saliva, tears, sweat, sputum, feces, urine, or blood.

### 2 . ONALABS

Provider of wearable devices to track body vitals and sports performance. It develops a non-invasive biomonitoring device to remotely analyze the health of newborn babies to the elderly for chronic heart failure and bronchiolitis. The device is embedded with chemical microsensors to detect the abnormality in the sweat like pH and glucose content for managing health deteriorations like myocardial infarctions and chronic lung diseases.

### 5 . Neuroelectrics

Neuroelectrics is a provider of wireless EEG monitoring and brain stimulation devices. It combines EEG monitoring and transcranial brain stimulation in wireless and non-invasive devices. The technology has applications in the management of neuropathic pain, computer-brain interfaces, and cognitive enhancement.
