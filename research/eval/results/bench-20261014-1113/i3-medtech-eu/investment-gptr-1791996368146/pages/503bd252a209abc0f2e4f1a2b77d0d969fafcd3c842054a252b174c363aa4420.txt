## About HealthTech sector in Spain
The HealthTech sector in Spain comprises 2.17K companies , including 396 funded companies having collectively raised $1.12B in venture capital money and private equity. Out of these, 83 are Series A+ funded. HealthTech sector in Spain has seen 37 acquisition s and 5 IPOs.
This sector includes companies providing digital health solutions for disease management, diagnosis, treatment etc .
HealthTech sector is also known as Digital Health, e-Health, Health And Wellness Tech, Smart Healthcare, Healthcare Technology or Health Management Technology.
It includes companies providing both B2B and B2C software, platforms and connected devices for providers, hospitals, consumers, employers, and life science industry. It excludes companies providing drugs, in vitro diagnostic solutions, drug delivery and medical devices that do not rely on digital platforms.

### 4 . Atrys

Atrys is a biomedical company that has developed a new approach to the management of cancer by integrating a molecular diagnostic methodology for individual tumor phenotyping that generates information which allows a specialist to identify which clinical approach is more appropriate for a patient and to identify those who can benefit from the single-dose stereotactic body radiation therapy (SD-SBRT or Single dose radiotherapy). Apart from the oncology offering, Atrys uses its proprietary telemedicine platform to provide its services in the medical specialities of radiology, cardiology, ophthalmology and dermatology.
