Spain's Wearable Technology sector has 94 tracked companies and $66M in disclosed funding, and if you're trying to find where the serious capital is concentrated, this directory ranks them by equity raised and recent funding momentum. Neuroelectrics leads the pack at $18M, with Homedoctor and Innitius each at $5M. No unicorns yet, but the pipeline is real. The Electronic Wearable Market puts the global opportunity at roughly $61.7B in 2023 scaling toward $466.5B by 2032 at a 15.9% CAGR, which means Spanish founders are building into a genuinely expanding space, not a crowded one. Europa, Lanzadera, and EIT Health are the most active backers here, which tells you something about where institutional conviction sits.

The hot themes right now are smartwatch and fitness-tracking form factors, and I'd argue Spain is better positioned than its funding totals suggest. Median app install growth is running at roughly +15% YoY across nearly 2,800 technology and health apps, a signal that consumer pull is outpacing coverage. A recent piece on eWear's European push shows a Spain-based player actively expanding across the continent, and separately, sign-language conversion wearables point to how far the use-case frontier is stretching. So coverage may be flat, but the underlying momentum isn't.
