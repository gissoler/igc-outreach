An innovative Spanish SME , a leader in the MedTech sector , has developed an advanced non-invasive cardiac monitor that promises to revolutionize the early detection and monitoring of cardiovascular diseases. With a granted European patent and CE Class IIa certification in its final stages, the company is actively seeking international strategic partners, including commercial distributors, e-health integrators, and venture capital investors, to expand its technology into key markets.

This cutting-edge technology addresses a critical need in the management of cardiovascular diseases, the leading cause of death worldwide, offering a more comprehensive and accessible solution than current methods.

Innovative technology: They have developed a non-invasive, portable heart monitor that uses a 30-second multi-sensor device (ECG, acoustic PCG, PPG) and a cloud-based AI engine. It allows for the capture of synchronized trimodal signals at home or in a doctor's office.

Solution to a Critical Problem: Unlike existing outpatient solutions, this device assesses the heart's mechanical performance, heart sounds, and extracts hemodynamic KPIs in real time, facilitating early diagnoses of heart failure and reducing rehospitalizations.

Clinical and Patient Benefits:

Prediction of the NTproBNP biomarker 15 days in advance.

Identification of heart failure events with the same margin.

Reduction of diagnosis time and improvement of patients' quality of life.

Reduced hospital costs and expenses for patients.

Contribution to the reduction of gender disparities in health and efficient health planning.

Advanced Development Status:

European patent granted for the sensor fusion hardware architecture.

Manufacturing license granted by the AEMPS.

CE Mark Class IIa certification in final review, with decision expected in the fourth quarter of 2026.

TRL 7-8 with proven clinical validation in multicenter trials in 8 hospitals and more than 100 patients.

Externally audited annual accounts, demonstrating financial rigor.

Search for Strategic Partners:

B2B Commercial Distributors and e-health Integrators: For hospital expansion and integration into electronic health record (EHR) systems, especially in cardiology, internal medicine, primary care, emergency departments, home telemonitoring, and insurance companies. Knowledge of reimbursement procedures and local regulations (ISO 13485) is valued.

Venture Capital Investors: A final Seed/Pre-Series A funding round of up to €800.000 (with investment tickets starting at €50.000) is being offered to complete the syndicate. We are seeking funds specializing in MedTech, HealthTech, or Life Sciences, Family Offices, Corporate Venture units, or experienced Business Angels.

Target Markets: The company is looking to expand into Iceland, Denmark, the United Kingdom, Brazil, Mexico, Norway, Switzerland, the United States, Italy, Colombia, Sweden, Singapore, Germany, France, Chile, Australia, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Finland, and Belgium.

Agreements Offered: Commercial, supply or investment collaborations.
