## 
Market Definition


The ECG sensor patches comprises lightweight, wearable devices that monitor a patient&rsquo;s heart activity by recording electrocardiogram (ECG) signals. These patches typically adhere to the chest and include integrated electrodes, sensors, and wireless transmitters to capture and send real-time cardiac data. Some versions may connect external electrode patches to a separate sensor unit or Holter monitor via lead wires, offering extended monitoring capabilities in a compact form. These products enable real-time, non-invasive cardiac monitoring. They are widely used for arrhythmia detection, post-operative surveillance, and remote patient management in modern cardiology and home-based healthcare settings.
