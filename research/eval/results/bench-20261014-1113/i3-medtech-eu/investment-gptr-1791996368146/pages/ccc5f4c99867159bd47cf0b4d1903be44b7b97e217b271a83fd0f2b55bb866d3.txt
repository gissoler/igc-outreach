# Idoven

idoven.ai ↗
· Madrid
· Series A

Idoven is a Madrid-based health technology company that builds an AI-powered "cardiology-as-a-service" platform for cardiovascular disease detection and precision medicine. Its CE-marked Class IIa product, Willem, automatically analyses ECGs from a wide range of devices to detect 22 cardiac patterns, serving medtech OEMs, pharma/life sciences (cardiotoxicity and risk stratification) and preventative screening programmes. Founded in 2018 out of research at Spain's CNIC, it raised a $12.9M Series A co-led by Insight Partners and Northzone in June 2022 (announced as $19.8M total with EIC Accelerator grant/equity), and counts athletes Iker Casillas and Pau Gasol among its angel backers.
