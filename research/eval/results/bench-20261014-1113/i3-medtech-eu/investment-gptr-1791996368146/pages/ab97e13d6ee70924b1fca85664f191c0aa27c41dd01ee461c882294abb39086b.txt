NM Angels announces recent investments in several startups

February 4, 2026 / In the News

NM Angels is pleased to announce recent investments in early-stage, high-growth companies across New Mexico. Notable investments include Sentiré Medical, CardiaCare, and Breezy Med. These investments reflect our ongoing commitment to backing innovation and supporting founders who are building companies that are making a difference in the state.

As New Mexico’s pre-eminent angel investment group, NM Angels connects accredited investors with promising startups and helps founders access not just capital, but experience, mentorship, and a growing community of investors, business people and founders.

In 2025 alone, NM Angels funded 19 companies, with $3.43MM invested and catalyzed, and 53+ checks written by our investor members.

The organization, which hosted over 50 events in 2025, offers numerous opportunities to get involved. New and experienced investors are invited to join New Angel Club , an annual 6-week program on the essentials of startup investing, starting April. Entrepreneurs are also invited to attend a variety of educational events focused on capital raising and more.

Learn more about the companies:

Sentiré Medical Systems’ patented Perf-Alert® is a revolutionary monitoring system that alerts surgeons to bowel gas during laparoscopic and robotic procedures, helping them operate more efficiently and safely.

CardiaCare is a breakthrough medical device company pioneering the world’s first therapeutic wearable device that uses noninvasive neurostimulation with AI-enabled continuous cardiac monitoring to detect, treat, and prevent Atrial Fibrillation events.

Breezy Med is a turnkey, touchless disinfection solution that enhances patient safety, improves operational efficiency, and reduces HAIs.

About NM Angels

New Mexico Angels is the state’s investor gathering place to support startup and high-growth companies. Members of the organization have invested $40MM+ in 80+ companies since 1999. Learn how to get involved !
