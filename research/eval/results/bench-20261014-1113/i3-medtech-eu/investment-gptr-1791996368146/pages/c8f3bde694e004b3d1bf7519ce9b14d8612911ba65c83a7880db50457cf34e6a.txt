# CRB Health Tech

Also known as CRB Inverbio / CRB Health Tech · CRB Inverbío

crbinverbio.com ↗
· Madrid
· Invests Seed – Series A
· Actively investing · last deal Aug 2026

CRB Health Tech (formerly CRB Inverbío) is a Madrid and Barcelona-based venture capital management firm founded in 2008 that invests in early-stage biotech, medtech and digital health startups. It has raised three funds — Biotech (Fund I), Medtech (Fund II) and CRB Digital Health III, which closed at €50M in 2025 — and counts Oncovision, Health in Code, Nuubo and Zoundream among its portfolio and exits.
