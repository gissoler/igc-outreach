### AI in Smart wearable ECG Monitors Market.

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is playing an increasingly pivotal role in the evolution of the smart wearable ECG monitors market. By enabling real-time analysis of ECG signals, AI algorithms can detect irregular heart rhythms such as atrial fibrillation, bradycardia, or tachycardia, with higher accuracy and faster response times than traditional methods. Advanced machine learning models are being integrated into wearable ECG devices to interpret vast amounts of physiological data, identify early signs of cardiac abnormalities, and reduce false positives that typically burden clinical workflows. In addition, AI-powered platforms facilitate personalized health insights by learning user-specific patterns and predicting potential cardiac events. This intelligence is particularly valuable for continuous monitoring in outpatient or home settings, enabling early intervention and reducing the need for emergency care. The integration of AI also supports seamless data sharing with electronic health records (EHRs), enhancing care coordination and contributing to broader digital health ecosystems.For instance, in June 2024, AliveCor received dual FDA clearance for its Kardia 12L ECG system, which utilizes the KAI 12L AI technology to identify 35 distinct cardiac conditions comprising 14 types of arrhythmias and 21 morphological patterns underscoring the growing role of AI in enhancing diagnostic precision within wearable ECG solutions.

## Case Study :

B-Secur, in collaboration with Fourth Frontier, carried out a clinical evaluation to test the effectiveness of their single-lead ECG chest strap device, the FX+, which uses B-Secur&rsquo;s HeartKey Core software. The purpose of the study was to determine how well the device could identify various heart rhythms such as normal sinus rhythm, atrial fibrillation, slow heart rate, and fast heart rate. The results from the device&rsquo;s automated analysis were compared against expert reviews of standard 12-lead ECGs and readings from the KardiaMobile device to verify accuracy.

Study Design:



82 adult patients participated in the study at an outpatient cardiology clinic.




Two consecutive sets of 30-second ECG recordings were obtained:



First set: FX+ and standard 12-lead ECG simultaneously.




Second set: FX+ and KardiaMobile device simultaneously.







The automated rhythm outputs from the FX+ were compared to the manual interpretations by cardiologists.




Key Findings:



The FX+ with HeartKey Core software demonstrated high accuracy:



96.8% sensitivity




94.3% specificity







A cardiologist determined that all FX+ ECG readings were equivalent to lead I of the concurrently recorded 12-lead ECG.




The FX+ device reported fewer inconclusive readings (11.9%)-where the ECG signal quality was insufficient for arrhythmia classification-compared to the KardiaMobile device (13.6%).




Conclusions:

The study concluded that the FX+ chest strap, when integrated with B-Secur&rsquo;s HeartKey Core software, provides accurate and reliable arrhythmia detection comparable to traditional 12-lead ECG systems. This validates the potential of single-lead ECG chest strap devices as effective tools for long-term cardiac monitoring and arrhythmia detection.

This system features customizable multi-modal dry-printed electrode patches capable of monitoring various biopotential signals across the body, including EEG, EOG, EKG/ECG, and EMG data.
