# Dawako Medtech

## Overview

Dawako Medtech is a Spanish medical-device company focused on wearable AI-bioimaging for digital health, precision medicine, and physiological performance monitoring. [1] The company was established in 2018 at the University of Valencia Science Park and is based in Paterna, Comunidad Valenciana, Spain. [2] Its stated mission is to give health professionals accessible tools for faster, more precise insight into patient health through ultrasound imaging, biosignals, and physiological-performance data. [3]

## Technology

Dawako's system combines wearable patch-format devices with an AI-powered cloud platform called PIIXMED. [3] The WBS and WBS-US patches are designed as wireless wearable systems that acquire ultrasound images and biosignals, including physiological and musculoskeletal data, even during motion. [4] PIIXMED is described as Software-as-a-Medical Device using proprietary AI and machine-learning algorithms to extract quantitative imaging and biosignal biomarkers from ultrasound images and physiological parameters. [5]

## Applications

The company's medical focus areas include musculoskeletal health, sarcopenia, clinical nutrition, sports medicine, and tissue characterization. [3] Dawako says its sports solution combines the WBS Patch with PIIXMED Sports to provide biomechanical data, advanced signal analysis, and remote access to training or competition data. [6] A 2023 Flexcon partnership announcement said Dawako and Flexcon were working to bring the WBS Patch and WBS-US Patch to market for uses including sarcopenia diagnosis support, clinical nutrition monitoring, and sports-medicine performance assessment. [7]

## Investors

Dawako says a group of private investors has invested, including entrepreneurs and health-sector professionals with experience in private hospital management, clinical experts in aging, nutrition, sports medicine and physiotherapy, clinical research participants, and professional athletes. [8] Publicly named institutional supporters in Dawako's own materials include CDTI, ENISA, AVI, and RED.es. [8] LinkedIn's Crunchbase-sourced funding panel identifies CEEI Valencia for a 16 November 2024 non-equity assistance event. [12] SpainCap identifies BeAble Capital as an investor in the EUR 0.999 million 2025 startup-stage equity financing and separately lists a 2025 minority startup investment by Xesgalicia. [13] PitchBook's public profile excerpt reports USD 5.52 million in total funding and names BeAble Capital, Vigo Activo, Red.es, INNVIERTE SICC, and National Innovation Company among the investors. [14]

## Status

Dawako appears active, with a live website, current product and investment pages, and public commercialization activity through its Flexcon collaboration. [3] It is best classified as a Medical Devices company because its core products are wearable ultrasound and biosignal patch systems paired with medical-device software. [7] There is no indication in the reviewed sources that the company has been acquired.
