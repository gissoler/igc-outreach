# Eoniq.fund

VC Fund

Sevilla, Spain

Find emails

Eoniq.fund is a generalist venture capital fund that invests in firms with Spanish based founders, being more proactive in terms of generating dealflow in regions outside of Madrid and Barcelona, and more reactive in those two hubs. They choose businesses with strong, ambitious teams, scalable business models, normally with a MVP already on themarket, and some traction, and are very centred on adding value to the startups that they invest in, over and above that of the funding itself.Eoniq.fund is headquartered in Sevilla, Andalucía.
