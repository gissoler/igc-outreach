ERA4Health JTC9 IICS4all 2027

ERA4Health JTC9 IICS4all 2027

ERA4Health

14.01.2027

Pre-proposal
ERA4Health's ninth Joint Transnational Call (JTC9), IICS4all 2027, will support multinational interventional investigator-initiated clinical studies addressing cardiovascular, metabolic, autoimmune and non-communicable respiratory diseases.
The call aims to generate high-quality clinical evidence through pragmatic, phase III, multinational interventional studies, while ensuring inclusivity and diversity in the study population and including sex/gender-sensitive, age-specific or other diversity-related aspects in the study. The call will support comparative-effectiveness studies and drug repurposing approaches involving approved off-patent medicines.

5th Innowwide call for market feasibility projects – Autumn 2026

Eureka 2026

EUREKA

01.12.2026

Innowwide is a mono beneficiary programme. The programme supports the organisation in the EU member state, Iceland, Israel, Norway, Switzerland, Türkiye, or United Kingdom, but entities in target countries that SMEs choose to collaborate with can benefit indirectly as subcontractors. The subcontractors are not party to the grant agreement i.e., they do not have a contractual relationship with the Funding Authority.
A market feasibility project includes preliminary technical, market, legal and/or socioeconomic assessments intended to validate the feasibility of your international and innovative business solution (product-, process- or service-market combination) within new and emerging global value chains.
The innovation to be explored in the market feasibility project must have a minimum Technology Readiness Level (TRL) of 4, meaning it has been validated in a laboratory or relevant environment and is ready for initial application or demonstration. The TRL of that innovation must not exceed 8 in your target country. Where further R&I activities are expected, TRL 7 is the indicative top limit.
