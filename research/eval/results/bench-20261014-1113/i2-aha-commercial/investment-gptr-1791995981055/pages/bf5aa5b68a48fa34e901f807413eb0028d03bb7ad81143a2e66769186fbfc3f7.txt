# Top 10 Pharma and Biotech Venture Capital Arms to Watch














## TLDR: Key Points

Pharma and biotech venture capital arms play a vital role in supporting biotech startups.

Companies like AbbVie Ventures, Astellas Venture Management, and JJDC are notable players in the industry.

Venture capital firms are crucial for helping biotech startups grow by providing financial and operational support. Some well-known names in the industry include AbbVie Ventures, Astellas Venture Management, and JJDC. These companies, along with others like Novartis Venture Fund, Novo Holdings, Pfizer Ventures, and Regeneron Ventures, invest in early-stage companies developing transformative therapies and technologies. Each firm has its unique focus areas and investment strategies, but they all share the goal of driving innovation in the biotech sector.

AbbVie Ventures, the corporate strategic venture capital arm of AbbVie, focuses on novel therapeutics in areas like oncology, immunology, and neuroscience. Astellas Venture Management, part of Astellas, supports cutting-edge science to enhance the company’s R&D pipeline. JJDC, the venture capital arm of Johnson & Johnson, invests across pharmaceuticals and medtech, leveraging the company’s expertise for portfolio companies.

Leaps by Bayer, M Ventures, Novartis Venture Fund, Novo Holdings, Pfizer Ventures, Regeneron Ventures, and other notable investors like Amgen, Bristol Myers Squibb, and Eli Lilly are actively shaping the biotech landscape with their investments. These venture capital arms offer funding, expertise, and strategic support to help startups bring innovative treatments to patients and drive progress in the biopharma industry.
