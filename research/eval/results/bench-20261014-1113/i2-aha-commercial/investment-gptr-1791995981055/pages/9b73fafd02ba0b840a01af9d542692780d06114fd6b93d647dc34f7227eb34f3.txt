Key Findings · 4 of 11 Flagged in Full Memo

D3 · Regulatory

STRENGTH

Regulatory pathway is coherent and articulated at founder level.

De Novo pathway is appropriate given no strong predicate exists for the community-hospital population. Pre-submission meeting with FDA scheduled within 60 days. Founder clinical literacy is tested immediately and passes.

D1 · Evidence

GAP

Clinical evidence is Tier 3, single-site.

One retrospective study at the pilot site, company-run, no peer review, no prospective validation. Acceptable at seed per framework, but must be closed before Series A. Target population generalizability is untested beyond the single pilot site.

D2 · Workflow

GAP

Single clinical champion carries the pilot.

The ICU-trained co-founder drives adoption at the 320-bed pilot site. Champion dependency is a scalability risk at seed and a deal-blocker at Series A. Workflow viability at a second, unrelated site is untested.

D4 · AI Validity

GAP

Model trained on single-site data; no bias assessment disclosed.

Training data comes entirely from the pilot site. No demographic stratification of performance published. Bias audit plan is described but not executed. External validation cohort not yet defined.

Verdict & Deal Structure

Proceed with structured milestones

Nimbus demonstrates the three things that matter most at seed: founder clinical literacy, a coherent regulatory pathway, and a defensible market positioning in a segment where existing solutions underperform. The clinical evidence gap and single-site concentration are expected at this stage. Price them with five structured milestones tied to the Series A decision. Milestone completion should be an IC gate at follow-on, not a courtesy check-in.

Recommended Milestones · Seed to Series A Bridge

By Close

Workflow

Second pilot site signed at a non-affiliated community hospital

Breaks single-champion dependency. Tests generalizability to a site the founding team has no prior relationship with.

03

Month 9

Evidence

Prospective validation study design IRB-approved, enrollment open

Moves evidence from Tier 3 (retrospective company-run) toward Tier 2 (prospective, peer-review-eligible). Primary endpoint: time-to-antibiotic and 48-hour escalation rate, not model accuracy.

04

Month 9

AI Validity

External validation cohort acquired and bias audit completed

Performance stratified by age, race, ethnicity, comorbidity burden, and site size. Results shared with investors regardless of outcome.

05

Month 12

Post-Deployment

Alert fatigue dashboard with clinician feedback loop deployed

Tracks clinician override rate, alert volume per shift, and time-to-action. Epic's sepsis model failed post-deployment on exactly these metrics.
