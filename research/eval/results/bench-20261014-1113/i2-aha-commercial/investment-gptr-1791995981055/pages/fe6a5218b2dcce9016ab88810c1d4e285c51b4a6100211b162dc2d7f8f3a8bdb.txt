### Why LPs Are Moving Into Emerging Venture Capital Funds
The largest brand-name funds are not where the outsized returns live. Research from the Colibri Institute analyzing nearly 2,500 venture funds raised between 2000 and 2024 found that emerging managers outperformed established firms on DPI, IRR, and TVPI. For LPs, three structural forces drive that edge:
Smaller fund math: A $5M to $10M fund returns real multiples on wins that would not move the needle at a $1B fund. Every position matters, and so does every LP.

Operator sourcing: These GPs lived inside the industries they invest in. A pharmacist sees pharmacy startups 18 to 24 months before generalists do. A quantum operator can tell the technical claim from the marketing.

First-check access: The companies that define a decade are funded years before the headlines. Emerging venture capital funds write that first check, at entry prices mega-funds never see.

Institutional capital has been crowding into brand names, which means LPs backing emerging venture capital funds today are getting better access and better terms precisely because everyone else stopped looking.

### How to Become an LP in Emerging Venture Capital Funds
Investing in emerging venture capital funds is more accessible than most accredited investors realize. Here is the path from browsing to committed capital.
Confirm you are an accredited investor. US securities rules generally require individual income above $200K in each of the past two years ($300K with a spouse) or net worth above $1M excluding your primary residence. Decile Access is built for accredited investors, and viewing fund materials requires no commitment.
Start with your thesis, not a fund name. You already have a view on how the next decade unfolds. Pick the belief first, whether that is the American industrial renaissance, applied AI in production, or emerging markets, then filter the marketplace by stage, geography, sector, and minimum check to find the managers placing the same bet.
Meet the Investment Lead directly. Every fund page offers a direct intro call with the Founder and Investment Lead. Use it to pressure-test the thesis, the sourcing edge, and the track record. These GPs answer their own calendars.
Understand the commitment. Venture is illiquid, with typical fund horizons of 8 to 10 years. Minimum checks on Decile Access start at $25K on select funds, which lets you build a diversified portfolio of emerging managers instead of concentrating in one.
