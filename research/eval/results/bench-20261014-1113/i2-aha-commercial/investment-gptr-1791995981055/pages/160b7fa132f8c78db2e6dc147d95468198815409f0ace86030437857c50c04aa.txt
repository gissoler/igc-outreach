From developing breakthrough therapies and applying artificial intelligence to drug discovery to manufacturing biologics and advancing precision medicine, biotech companies are transforming how diseases are understood, treated, and managed.

These organizations have been grouped based on the role they play within the biotechnology ecosystem, providing a broad view of the companies driving innovation across drug discovery, clinical development, manufacturing, precision medicine, and life sciences technology.

Roche is one of the world&#x27;s largest biotechnology companies and a global leader in pharmaceuticals and diagnostics. Headquartered in Switzerland, the company has played a major role in advancing precision medicine by combining therapeutic development with diagnostic technologies. Roche has a particularly strong presence in oncology, neuroscience, ophthalmology, immunology, and rare diseases, and continues to invest heavily in personalized healthcare and next-generation therapies.
The company&#x27;s integrated approach to pharmaceuticals and diagnostics has helped it maintain a leading position in the global life sciences industry, with innovations spanning biologics, targeted therapies, and molecular diagnostics.

In addition to its diabetes and obesity portfolio, Novo Nordisk continues to invest in cardiovascular disease, rare diseases, and next-generation biologics. Its expertise in peptide therapeutics, biologics manufacturing, and metabolic research has positioned it as one of the most influential biotechnology companies in the world.

## Frequently asked questions about biotech companies

### 1. What are biotech companies?
Biotech companies use biology, genetics, chemistry, and technology to develop products and services that improve human health, agriculture, industrial processes, and scientific research. In healthcare, biotechnology companies focus on areas such as drug discovery, biologics, gene therapies, diagnostics, and precision medicine.

### 2. What is the difference between biotechnology and pharmaceutical companies?
Traditional pharmaceutical companies have historically focused on developing drugs using chemical compounds, while biotechnology companies often use biological systems, genetic engineering, cell therapies, and advanced technologies to develop treatments. Today, however, many organizations operate across both pharmaceutical and biotechnology domains.

### 3. Which are the largest biotech companies in the world?
Some of the largest and most influential biotech companies include Roche, Novo Nordisk, Amgen, AstraZeneca, Gilead Sciences, Regeneron, and Vertex Pharmaceuticals. These organizations lead the industry across therapeutics, diagnostics, precision medicine, and biologics.

### 4. How is artificial intelligence changing the biotechnology industry?
Artificial intelligence is helping biotechnology companies accelerate drug discovery, identify therapeutic targets, design molecules, analyze clinical data, and optimize research and development workflows. Companies such as Recursion, Insilico Medicine, Isomorphic Labs, and Generate are leading this transformation.

### 5. What are CROs and CDMOs in biotechnology?
Contract research organizations (CROs) help biotechnology companies conduct clinical trials, manage research programs, and support regulatory activities. Contract development and manufacturing organizations (CDMOs) provide manufacturing, process development, and commercialization services for biologics, cell therapies, and pharmaceutical products.

### 6. What technologies are shaping the future of biotechnology?
Some of the most important technologies shaping biotechnology include artificial intelligence, gene editing, cell and gene therapies, precision medicine, genomics, next-generation sequencing, biologics manufacturing, and cloud-based scientific research platforms.
