# Pfizer Ventures
New York, New York, United States · Founded 2004
www.pfizer.com/about/partners/venture-investments

Biotech & Life Sciences Healthcare
Pfizer Ventures is the strategic corporate venture capital arm of Pfizer Inc., founded in 2004. It invests globally in innovative biopharmaceutical companies advancing breakthrough medicines, platform technologies, and other healthcare technologies of current or future strategic interest to Pfizer.

50% of their portfolio is in Biotech & Life Sciences. Deal activity increased 117% year-over-year (13 deals in the last 12 months). Average disclosed round size is $64.9M (across 159 rounds with reported amounts).
