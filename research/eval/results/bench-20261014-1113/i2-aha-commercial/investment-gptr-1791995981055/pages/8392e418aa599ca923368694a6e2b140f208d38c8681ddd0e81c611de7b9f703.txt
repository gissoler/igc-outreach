Strategics are buying optionality at seed now, not Series B.

Methodology
This tracker covers biotech and biopharma funding rounds and other company financings announced or first publicly disclosed in 2026. Inclusion is not limited to private venture-capital equity rounds: it also includes crossover and pre-IPO financings, launch or spinout capitalisations, selected strategic or royalty-based private investments, and disclosed non-dilutive programme funding or grants. Reverse-merger-linked financings are included where they provide capital to an eligible biotech or biopharma company. For non-equity funding, the tracker identifies the funding type and distinguishes an initial or committed amount from optional future contract ceilings; it does not present contingent maximums as capital already raised. Round sizes are shown as disclosed; where multiple tranches or currencies are reported, both are shown. Deals with no publicly disclosed amount are excluded. Records are sorted newest first within each month.
Crossover and pre-IPO rounds sit here; completed listings are covered separately in the biotech IPO tracker . For how tranches, syndicates and investor rights work, see tranched financing and syndicate terms .
