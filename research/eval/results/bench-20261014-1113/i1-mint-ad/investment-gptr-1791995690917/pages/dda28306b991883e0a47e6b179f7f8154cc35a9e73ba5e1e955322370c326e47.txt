# JuneBrain

## Overview

JuneBrain is a Maryland-founded medical technology company developing portable optical coherence tomography (OCT) devices and AI software for remote monitoring of neurological and retinal diseases. [1] The company describes its products as investigational-use-only and positions them for clinical, point-of-care, and research settings where more frequent retinal imaging could support neurology and ophthalmology care. [1] JuneBrain is headquartered in Baltimore, Maryland, with its recent ecosystem support centered around Baltimore's LaunchPort and regional commercialization programs. [2]

## Technology

The company's flagship system is Neuro-i SS-OCT, a wearable, AI-powered retinal imaging platform designed to bring high-resolution OCT imaging beyond traditional eye clinics into neurology, primary care, community health, and mobile settings. [2] JuneBrain also offers a Reading Center service focused on automated quantitative analysis of OCT data for clinical research. [1] In a SEC filing, the company described Neuro-i as a mobile, wearable device for remote detection of ophthalmologic or brain disease, paired with cloud software that segments and analyzes retinal OCT images using deep-learning-based methods. [3]

## Products and Development

JuneBrain's Neuro-i SS-OCT is intended to detect and monitor eye and brain disease through technician-free retinal imaging. [2] ETC Baltimore reported in 2026 that the company had built and tested working Neuro-i SS-OCT prototypes and enrolled nearly 100 patients across three company-sponsored clinical studies. [2] The same report said JuneBrain was advancing toward FDA submission in 2026 and had multiple issued patents supporting its device and algorithm technology. [2]

## Clinical Focus

JuneBrain targets diseases where retinal imaging may help quantify progression or treatment response, including multiple sclerosis, Parkinson's disease, Alzheimer's disease, and retinal disorders. [4] NSF's description of the company's STTR Phase II project emphasized development of a fully automated, wearable, lower-cost retinal imaging device for clinical and home settings, initially highlighting multiple sclerosis monitoring. [5] The company frames its broader goal as expanding access to advanced eye and brain health monitoring across underserved and non-traditional care environments. [1]

JuneBrain has received non-dilutive and private financing to support product development and commercialization. ETC Baltimore reported in February 2026 that the company had secured $3 million in grants, including Phase I and II NSF SBIR/STTR awards, and raised $3.4 million in private investment. ## Investors and Partners

Recent investors and supporters include Emerging Technology Centers Baltimore, TEDCO, The New Orleans BioFund, and Gulf South Angels. [2] [4] CityBiz reported in April 2026 that the New Orleans BioFund and Gulf South Angels made a joint investment to help JuneBrain expand into Louisiana and deepen clinical partnerships with regional health systems and universities. [4] TEDCO had previously invested $250,000 through its Social Impact Funds, according to the same report. [4]
