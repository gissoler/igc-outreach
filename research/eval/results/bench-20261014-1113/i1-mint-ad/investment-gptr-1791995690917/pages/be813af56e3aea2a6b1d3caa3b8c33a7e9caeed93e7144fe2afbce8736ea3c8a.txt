# Recently Funded Startups in Maryland

Updated Oct 2026
FundedIQ holds 382 funded startups in Maryland and keeps their funding rounds, amounts, investors, industries, locations and company details up to date. This page provides an overview of the startup funding landscape in Maryland, along with the 100 most recently funded startups there.
The median disclosed round is $4.5M, with the middle half of rounds falling between $500K and $35M. Biotechnology, Health Care and Artificial Intelligence hold the most companies by sector. Baltimore and Bethesda hold the deepest concentrations of funded startups in Maryland. Startups in Maryland closed 173 funding rounds over the last 24 months, 80 of them in the last 12 months, and raised $14B in disclosed capital.
Biotechnology and Health Care dominate the local profile. Early stage labels and TEDCO involvement indicate a heavy concentration of young ventures. These companies are typically small teams in the initial stages of product development. This creates a target audience of early adopters and technical partners rather than mature corporate buyers.

## About the FundedIQ Maryland startup funding data

FundedIQ tracks announced startup funding rounds and organises them by company, funding
date, stage, amount, industry, location and investor. The dataset is refreshed weekly, and
new rounds are normalised and de-duplicated before they are published, so the same round
reported twice on different dates is recorded once.

Figures on this page cover the 382 funded startups in Maryland currently tracked.
Round counts, capital totals and the median round are computed over a trailing 24 months.
Rounds whose amount was not disclosed (38 of 173 in that period) are counted as rounds but excluded from capital totals and from the median. The most recent round on record for funded startups in Maryland is dated Oct 2026.
