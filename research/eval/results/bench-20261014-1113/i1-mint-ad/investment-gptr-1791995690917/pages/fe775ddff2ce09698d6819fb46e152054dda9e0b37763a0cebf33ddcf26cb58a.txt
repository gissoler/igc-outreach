# Bio + Healthcare

Innovations at the intersection of technology and biology will drive major improvements in the quality of our health and the cost of healthcare.

## What we do

### We Invest in Digital Health and Novel Life Science Companies

Menlo Ventures invests at the intersection of bio and tech, with a differentiated approach in core thesis areas including therapeutic platforms, transformative technologies, digital health, and healthcare SaaS.

Today, AI can predict how proteins fold, design new drugs, act as an ambient scribe during doctor visits, and more, leading to better treatments, tools, and diagnostics—more quickly and cost-effectively than ever before.

## Our Perspective

At Menlo Ventures, we believe many of the greatest innovations in healthcare and life sciences will result from multi-disciplinary efforts that combine the best of biology and technology.
