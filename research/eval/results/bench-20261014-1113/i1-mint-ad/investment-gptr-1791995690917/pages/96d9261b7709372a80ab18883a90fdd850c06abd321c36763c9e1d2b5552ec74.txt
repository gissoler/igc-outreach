### July 2026

#### SoundHealth
Date: July 30, 2026
Series: Series A (oversubscribed)
Amount: $12.25M
Therapeutic area: Nasal congestion, allergic/non-allergic rhinitis, and sleep-related breathing disorders
Modality: FDA-cleared AI-powered wearable device used to treat allergic and non-allergic rhinitis.
Notable Investors: Shangbay Capital; Moai Capital; J4 Ventures; TeleSoft Partners
  Located in Los Altos, California, SoundHealth announced that it closed an oversubscribed Series A round to bring its total venture funding to $12.25M. The company plans to use the funds to improve its growth trajectory for its FDA-cleared wearable device, Sonu, which is an alternative to corticosteroid nasal sprays used for allergic and non-allergic rhinitis. The device delivers targeted acoustic vibrational waves via the wearable band that stimulate autonomic nerve pathways and increase nitric oxide release in the nasal mucosa, reducing tissue swelling and congestion. In addition to Sonu, the company also makes Spatial Sleep, a personalized acoustic device for sleep onset and duration.

### June 2026

#### GT Medical Technologies
Date: June 9, 2026
Series: Series E
Amount: $100M
Therapeutic area: Brain tumors 
Modality: Radiotherapy implant
Notable Investors: Viking Global Investors, MVM Partners, Gilde Healthcare, Evidity Health Capital, Medtech Venture Partners, FemHealth Ventures
Tempe, Ariz.-based GT Medical Technologies oversubscribed $100 Million Series E equity financing to advance commercial treatment for patients with operable brain tumors with its GammaTile, an FDA-cleared bioresorbable radiotherapy implant placed directly into the brain tumor cavity during surgery. The capital will fund commercial expansion and the BRIDGES trial for newly diagnosed glioblastoma patients. The company’s ROADS randomized trial that was presented at the 2026 ASCO conference showed GammaTile reduced tumor recurrence risk by 93% and death risk by 41% at 12 months versus standard care.

#### Butterfly Medical
Date: May 14, 2026
Series: Series C
Amount: $21M
Therapeutic area: Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH)
Modality: Implantable device
Investors: European Innovation Council (EIC), Junson Capital, MedVenture, New Rhein Healthcare Investors
Based in Boston, Mass., and Yokneam, Israel, Butterfly Medical  secured $21 Million in upsized Series C funding to advance its minimally Invasive BPH treatment, which is designed to alleviate the common and debilitating symptoms of BPH. The device already secured CE Mark approval for use in Europe, and is a minimally invasive, implantable solution that mechanically retracts the lateral lobes of the prostate to restore urine flow. It is made to restore and maintain natural urinary flow without the need for cutting, burning or removing tissue, while also being fully reversible.
