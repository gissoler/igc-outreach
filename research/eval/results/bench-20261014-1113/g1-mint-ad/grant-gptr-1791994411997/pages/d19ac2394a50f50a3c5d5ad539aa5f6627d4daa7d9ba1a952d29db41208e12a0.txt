### Maryland Innovation Initiative (MII) - Technology Assessment Phase

Maryland Innovation Initiative (MII) - Technology Assessment Phase is a grant from TEDCO (Maryland Technology Development Corporation) that funds commercialization of research technologies developed at Maryland&#x27;s five qualifying universities: Johns Hopkins University, Morgan State University, University of Maryland Baltimore, University of Maryland Baltimore County, and University of Maryland College Park. The program promotes the transition of promising technologies with significant commercial potential from university labs to the commercial sector through technology validation, market assessment, and startup formation. MII expanded in FY23 to include new opportunities, and funding levels vary by phase. Eligible applicants are faculty, researchers, and technology transfer offices at the five participating Maryland academic institutions pursuing commercialization of university-generated intellectual property.
Up to $130,000 for a sole applicant; up to $180,000 for joint applications Deadline: Check Source
