### Maryland Innovation Initiative (MII) - Company Formation

Maryland Innovation Initiative (MII) - Company Formation is sponsored by TEDCO. The Maryland Innovation Initiative (MII) program is designed to foster the commercialization of promising technologies with significant commercial potential from qualifying universities to the commercial sector. The Company Formation phase specifically supports Maryland-based start-up companies that have licensed technology from a qualifying university within twelve months of submitting a proposal.
