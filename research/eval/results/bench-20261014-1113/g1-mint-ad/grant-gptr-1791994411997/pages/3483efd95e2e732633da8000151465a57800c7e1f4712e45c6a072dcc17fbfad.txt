## What if we trained the next generation in the ARPA model of innovation and bold thinking?

### The Problem
ARPA-H needs bold vision and creative thinking to solve America’s most pressing biotech and healthcare challenges. Game-changing ideas in healthcare start small—in a lab, a startup, or a young team that lacks critical funding. Currently, there is not an established agency program to develop and mentor early-career researchers. This limits who we reach and who benefits from our programs. Early-career investigators bring fresh perspectives on the front lines of health research but are not as likely to apply for ARPA-H funding opportunities.

### The Solution
ARPA-H’s Emerging Health Innovators (EHI) Initiative aims to increase access to government research funding and to seed new ideas that address healthcare gaps in the United States. The program is designed for early-career biotech investigators and innovators to learn how to become the next wave of ARPA-H Program Managers and research performers. By backing emerging talent, EHI ensures the next big health breakthroughs don’t get stuck on the drawing board.
ARPA-H intends EHI to offer multiple Topic Areas of interest across the ARPA-H mission offices. The solicitation will be published annually and anticipates funding 20 or more awards per year.

## EHI Solicitation: 2026 Cycle
Notice ID: ARPA-H-SOL-26-150
Proposals for this opportunity must be led by early-career principal investigators (academic, non-profit, industry etc.) within 8 years after completion of their first terminal degree.
The 2026 cycle will seek proposals across these topic areas:
Precision Control of Skin Inflammation without Systemic Exposure
Internal Sensing and Targeted Control of Inflammation
Deep Brain Stimulation for Mechanistic Sleep Exploration (Deep Sleep)
CLEAR-MIND — Restoring the Brain's Drainage and Immune Balance to Enable Healing After Injury
Host-Pathway Interventions for Neurotropic Disease (HIND)
Protecting Patient Data in Life Science AI
Engineering DRT for Programmable DNA Synthesis as Emerging New Medical Countermeasures (MCM) Strategy
Prevention of Lethal Alpha-gal Allergic Responses Caused by Tick Bites
SMILE (Smart Microbiome-Informed Lesion & Enamel restoration)
Ending Preventable Deaths from Ectopic Pregnancy Through First-Visit Diagnosis
Transforming the Well-Woman Exam through Objective Data
Innovations in Biotransport to Support Long-Term Preservation of Tissues and Organs
Organs-in-Action: Next-Generation Human Tissue Platforms for Longevity, Disease, and Therapy Discovery
Near Real-time, Non-contact Determination of Sterility in Distributed Manufacturing Systems
ICAT (In-Cavity Adjuvant Therapy): Implantable Systems for Local Cancer Control at the Resection Margin
Causally-Grounded Alignment for Clinical Generative Artificial Intelligence Tools
To learn more about this opportunity, ARPA-H invites interested parties to download the official solicitation documents, which are posted and maintained on SAM.gov.
