# Limited Competition: NIH Research Evaluation and Commercialization Hubs (REACH) (SB0 Clinical Trial Optional) (RFA-OD-27-009)

Sponsor: National Institutes of Health

Award: The primary applicant organization must be a university or other research institution that participates in the NIH Small Business Technology Transfer (STTR) program. Participation means that a university or other research institution has been a formal partner to a small business on an STTR award. The maximum budget for a Hub is $1,000,000 total costs per year. The maximum project period is 4 years.

Due Date: November 10, 2026

# BRAIN Initiative: Research Opportunities Using Invasive Neural Recording and Stimulating Technologies in the Human Brain (R01 Clinical Trial Not Allowed) (RFA-NS-28-010)

Sponsor: National Institutes of Health

Award: Application budgets need to reflect the actual needs of the proposed project. It is anticipated that most awards will not exceed $1M direct costs. The maximum project period is 5 years.

Due Date: Multiple due dates; see announcement.

# Kissick Family Foundation Frontotemporal Dementia (FTD) Grant Program

Sponsor: The Kissick Family Foundation and the Milken Institute’s Science Philanthropy Accelerator for Research and Collaboration (SPARC)

Award: The program supports fundamental science and early-stage translational research into sporadic FTD, and awards two-year grants of up to $500,000, inclusive of indirect costs. Early-career and newly independent investigators are a priority for this cycle.

Due Date: Letters of intent due November 4, 2026. An information session will be held October 13, 2026, 11 a.m. ET.

# Limited Competition: Instrumentation Grant Program for Resource-Limited Institutions (S10 - Clinical Trial Not Allowed) (PAR-27-067)

Sponsor: National Institutes of Health

Award: Since different instruments will range in cost, the amount of the award will vary. The minimum award is $25,000 and, although there is no upper limit on the cost of the instrument, the maximum award is $250,000. No indirect costs are allowed for S10 awards. Awards are made for one year only.

Due Date: Multiple due dates; see announcement.

# Predoctoral to Postdoctoral Transition Award (Parent F99/K00 - Clinical Trial Not Allowed) (PA-27-037)

Sponsor: National Institutes of Health

Award: Award budgets are composed of stipends, tuition and fees, and institutional allowance. Individuals may receive up to 5 years of aggregate Kirschstein-NRSA support at the predoctoral level (up to 6 years for dual degree training, e.g., MD/PhD), and up to 3 years of aggregate Kirschstein-NRSA support at the postdoctoral level, including any combination of support from institutional training grants (e.g., T32) and an individual fellowship award.

Due Date: Multiple due dates; see announcement.
