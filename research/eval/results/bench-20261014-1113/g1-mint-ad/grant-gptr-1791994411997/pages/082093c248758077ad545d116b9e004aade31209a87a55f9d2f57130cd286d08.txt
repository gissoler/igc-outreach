# Business Funding & Incentives

Montgomery County has a wide range of funding and incentive programs which may help you grow your business. Some of these are offered in partnership with local, state, or federal agencies. Others are specific to certain industries, small businesses, or Minority, Women, or Disabled-owned businesses.

Reach out to our Business Center if you have questions or need help.

## Funding to Help You Build/Expand/Renovate Your Facility
These funds may help you build your new or expanded facility in Montgomery County. Often Montgomery County will partner with the State of Maryland or your city to offer you a joint award.

### Montgomery County
Funding: Day Care Provider Tax Credit
This program is for: 
Taxpayers wanting to make improvements on a real property that is used exclusively for day care services (registered family day care home, licensed childcare center, licensed day care center for the elderly, or licensed day care center for the medically handicapped).
Taxpayers wanting to make improvements on a real property that is used exclusively for day care services and is owned by a business with at least 25 employees
Funding:  Design for Life (DFL) Property Tax Incentive Program
This program is for:  Home care facility owners wanting to make their home more universally accessible.
Funding:  Economic Development Grant and Loan Program
This program is for:  Private employers who retain jobs or stimulate job creation in Montgomery County. Priority is given to:
High technology companies
Manufacturing companies
Businesses in urban revitalization areas
Private employers providing the greatest public benefits
Funding:  Energy-Efficient Buildings Property Tax Credits
This program is for:  The County’s Energy-Efficient Buildings program is a two-tiered incentive of property tax credits for new and existing energy-efficient commercial and multi-family buildings. Existing building owners can earn up to a 100% property tax credit against County taxes for two years for eligible energy efficiency improvements. Newly constructed building owners can earn up to a 100% property tax credit against County taxes for four years for constructing a new building exceeding current building code requirements. 
See more DEP incentives and rebates
Funding:  Enterprise Zone Tax Credit
This program is for:  Businesses in State-designated Enterprise Zones (Long Branch/Takoma Park, Glenmont, Burtonsville, Briggs Chaney, and Olde Towne Gaithersburg).
Funding:  Façade Improvement Program
This program is for:  Property owners and business owners whose exteriors are older than 10 years or don’t meet today’s commercial standards.
Funding:  Group Home Rehabilitation Loan Program
This program is for:  Group homes owned by nonprofit organizations which are occupied by persons of low- and moderate-income who are disabled.
Funding:  Historic Preservation Tax Credit
This program is for:  Businesses that want to renovate properties listed in the Montgomery County Master Plan for Historic Preservation.
Funding:  Job Creation Fund
This program is for:  Employers in the following industries who will create at least five new jobs each paying $100,000 or more within a six month period:
Advanced Technology
Cybersecurity
Hospitality
Life Sciences
Quantum Computing Technology
Funding:  MOVE Program
This program is for:  Businesses that are signing their first commercial lease for office space or expanding their existing office space lease by at least 500 square feet. This program is not for retail, restaurant, independent financial or insurance agent/broker establishments.
Funding:  Multi-family Housing Projects
This program is for:  Developers and property managers who want to develop and rehabilitate multi-family housing properties.
Funding:  Multi-family Energy Incentives
This program is for:  Multi-family property owners who want to reduce the energy costs of their properties.
Funding:  New Jobs Tax Credit
This program is for:  Businesses that increase their space by at least 5,000 square feet and their employee count by at least 25 new jobs.
 

### Maryland
Funding:  Advantage Maryland (MEDAAF)
This program is for:  Businesses that support economic development initiatives. Projects must include a County Resolution. This program is for certain eligible industry sectors.
Funding:  Data Center Maryland Sales and Use Tax Exemption Incentive Program
This program is for:  Businesses that locate or expand in Maryland and create new full-time positions. As part of the eligibility criteria a business must be certified as a Qualified Data Center by the Maryland Department of Commerce, along with other criteria.
Funding:  Enterprise Zone Tax Credits
This program is for:  Businesses in a Maryland enterprise zone in return for job creation and investments. Businesses in Focus Areas may be also qualified for personal property tax credits on new investment in personal property and enhanced income tax credit for creating new jobs.
Funding:  Job Creation Tax Credit
This program is for:  Businesses that locate or expand in the state and create a minimum number of net new full-time positions. The credit increases if the business locates or expands in a revitalization area. The business must be certified as a qualified business entity eligible for the tax credit.
Funding:  Maryland Economic Adjustment Fund (MEAF)
This program is for:  Businesses that have an inability to qualify for loans from traditional lending resources might be eligible for this loan program. The business must demonstrate credit worthiness and ability to repay the obligation.
Funding:  Maryland Opportunity Zones
This program is for:  Businesses that are new or expanding might qualified for the Opportunity Zone Fund. There are a variety of funds available with different investment focus.
Funding:  Maryland Opportunity Zone Enhancement Credits
This program is for:  Businesses that are in Maryland Opportunity Zones which provides enhancements to several economic development tax credits. The tax credits include job creation, one Maryland Tax Credit that creates at least 50 qualified positions, more jobs for Marylanders, Enterprise Zone Income Tax Credit, Enterprise Zone Focus Area Income Tax Credit, Biotechnology Investor Incentive Tax Credit, and Maryland Innovation Investment Tax Credit.
Funding:  More Jobs for Marylanders
This program is for:  Businesses that are involved in manufacturing, but also non-manufacturer business are eligible if they are located or expand in opportunity zones. The State sets an application period after July 1 each year.
 

### City of Gaithersburg
Funding:  Economic Development Toolbox
This program is for:  Businesses moving to or expanding within the City of Gaithersburg, that have been in operation for two or more years. Matching grants for capital improvements.
Funding:  Economic Development Opportunity Fund
This program is for:  Larger businesses wanting to relocate or expand within the City of Gaithersburg. Olde Towne Enterprise Zone Businesses in the designated areas of Olde Towne Gaithersburg and the Frederick Avenue commercial corridor. Tax credits for investment in real property and new job creation.
 

### City of Rockville
Funding:  Business Expansion Fund
This program is for:  Existing Rockville businesses expanding their operations within the corporate limits of the City of Rockville.
Funding:  MOVE Program
This program is for: 
Businesses who are outside the corporate limits of the City of Rockville and want to relocate or expand into Rockville.
Startups moving into their first commercial space and it is in Rockville.
 

### City of Takoma Park
Funding:  Open for Business Grant Program
This program is for:  Businesses looking to locate, grow, or expand in the City of Takoma Park.
 

## Funding to Help You Commercialize Your Technology
Several local, state and federal programs are available to help businesses advance their proprietary technology ideas to market.

### Montgomery County
Funding:  Biotechnology Investor Incentive Program
This program is for:  Investors of biotechnology companies in Montgomery County. Investors who are approved and receive tax credits from the Maryland Biotechnology Investment Incentive Tax Credit Program will automatically receive the supplemental grant from the County.
 

### Maryland
Funding:  Biotechnology Investment Incentive Tax Credit
This program is for:  Investors with eligible investments in a Qualified Maryland Biotechnology Company (QMBC). The program supports investment in seed and early stage biotech companies to promote and grow the biotech industry in Maryland.
Funding:  Enterprise Zone Tax Credits
This program is for:  Businesses in a Maryland enterprise zone in return for job creation and investments. Businesses in Focus Areas may be also qualified for personal property tax credits on new investment in personal property and enhanced income tax credit for creating new jobs. 
Funding:  Innovation Incentive Tax Credit
This program is for:  Potential investors in early-stage companies with the goal of increasing the number of companies developing innovative technologies in Maryland, increasing overall investments in current and emerging technology sectors, and increasing the number of individual investors actively investing in Maryland technology companies.
Funding:  Maryland Industrial Partnerships (MIPS)
This program is for:  Companies looking for funding, facilities, resources, and expertise within Maryland’s public universities to create new products and opportunities.
Funding:  Maryland Innovation Initiative
This program is for:  Start-ups within:
Johns Hopkins University
Morgan State University
University of Maryland, Baltimore
University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC)
University of Maryland, College Park
Funding:  Momentum Fund
This program is for:  Early-stage companies that are affiliated with the University System of Maryland (USM) .
Funding:  Research and Development Tax Credit
This program is for:  Businesses that incur Qualified Research and Development Expenses, as defined by § 41(b) of the Internal Revenue Code, in Maryland.
Funding:  Stem Cell Research Fund
This program is for:  Companies, universities/research institutes, and academics involved in a variety of stem cell research in Maryland can apply for grants to help them advance their technologies.
Funding:  Venture Fund
This program is for:  Early-stage technology businesses in Maryland.
 

### Federal
Funding: America’s Seed Fund (SBIR/STTR)
This program is for:  Startups and small businesses across technology areas and markets to stimulate technological innovation, meet Federal research and development (R&D) needs, and increase commercialization to transition R&D into impact.
 

## Funding to Help You Grow or Train Your Workforce
These programs offer financial support to grow or train your workforce.

### Montgomery County
Funding:  Home Computer Telecommuting Incentive
This program is for:  Businesses in Montgomery County that establish a new off-site employee workstation for employees who work at home at least 78 days per calendar year.
Funding:  Job Creation Fund
This program is for:  Employers in the following industries who will create at least five new jobs each paying $100,000 or more within a six month period:
Advanced Technology
Cybersecurity
Hospitality
Life Sciences
Quantum Computing Technology
Funding:  New Jobs Tax Credit
This program is for:  Businesses in Montgomery County that are planning to increase both their space and their staff.
 

### Maryland
Funding:  Employer Security Clearances Costs (ESCC) Tax Credit
This program is for:  Small businesses in Maryland doing security-based contract work.
Funding:  Hire Our Veterans Tax Credit
This program is for:  Small businesses in Maryland hiring military veterans.
Funding:  Job Creation Tax Credit
This program is for: 
Businesses that locate or expand in Maryland and create a minimum number of net new full-time positions.
Small businesses that hire at least one qualified veteran employee to fill a net new full-time position.
Funding:  Maryland Apprenticeship and Training Program (MATP)
This program is for:  Businesses and apprenticeship providers committed to creating new apprenticeship opportunities to meet their workforce needs.
Funding:  Maryland Business Works
This program is for:  Businesses that want to train their employees.
 

### City of Gaithersburg
Funding:  Economic Development Toolbox
This program is for:  Existing businesses located within the City of Gaithersburg that have been in operation for more than two years
 

## Funding to Help You Grow Your Minority-owned Businesses
Several local, state, and federal programs focus on providing resources to small businesses and businesses owned by people from historically disadvantaged groups. For non-funding related resources for small businesses and minority, women, and veteran-owned businesses, visit our page on Resources for Small, Minority, Women, and Veteran-owned Businesses .

### Montgomery County
Funding: MicroLoan
This program is for:  Program Start-ups, newly established, or growing small businesses and certain not-for-profit childcare centers. 
These loans currently are provided through these County partners:
Latino Economic Development Center
Life Asset
 

### Rockville
Funding:  Rockville Small Business Impact Fund (PDF)
This program is for:  Small businesses in one of Rockville’s Performance Districts:
Town Center Performance District
Twinbrook Metro Performance District
Nonprofit businesses are eligible. Franchise or chain businesses are not eligible.
 

### Maryland
Funding:  Accelerating Community Excellence (ACE) Loan Fund
This program is for:  Minority- and women-owned small businesses.
Funding:  Social Impact Funds (Pre-Seed Builder and Inclusion)
This program is for:  Pre-seed technology start-ups from all industries who meet the following qualifications:
Main business location needs to be in Maryland
At least one full-time employee
50% or more of founders demonstrate economic disadvantage
Funding:  State Small Business Credit Initiative
This program is for:  Startups and entrepreneurs who demonstrate economic or social disadvantage.
 

## Funding to Support Your Clean Energy/Sustainability-related Activities
Funding may be available from local, state, and federal sources and utility companies to support your business’ adoption of energy-efficient practices and renewable technologies.

### Montgomery County
Funding:  Commercial Energy Incentives
This program is for:  Commercial building owners. This resource has information on energy assessments, financial incentives, and government programs and incentives.
Funding:  Energy and Environmental Design (Green Building) Tax Credit
This program is for:  Building owners whose building or reconstruction project achieve a LEEDS certification.
Funding:  Fire Sprinkler System Tax Credit
This program is for:  Commercial building owners the first time they install an approved complete automatic sprinkler system that is used for fire protection.
Funding:  Fuel Energy Tax Exemption for Onsite Clean Energy
This program is for:  Commercial building owners whose buildings generate electricity through onsite solar installations. 
Funding:  Property-Assessed Clean Energy (C-PACE)
This program is for:  Commercial building owners wanting to make building improvements.
Funding:  Water Quality Protection Charge Credit
This program is for:  Property owners who own and maintain on-site stormwater management practices.
 

### City of Rockville
Funding:  Green Building Tax Credit
This program is for: 
Property owners who want to renovate their buildings to meet LEED Silver, Gold, or Platinum certification and ENERGY STAR certification.
Existing commercial buildings and multifamily residential structures with 4 or more stories.
 

### Maryland
Funding:  Jane E. Lawton Conservation Loan Program
This program is for:  Nonprofit organizations, local governments, businesses, and State agencies in Maryland for the implementation of cost-effective energy efficiency and conservation improvements for existing or to-be-constructed facilities.
Funding:  Maryland Clean Energy Capital Financing Program
This program is for:  Governments, institutions, nonprofit organizations, and business entities wanting to achieve favorable economics in energy project transactions.
Funding:  Open Energy Grant Program
This program is for:  People who have ideas for energy-related projects that fall outside the range of the Maryland Energy Administration’s (MEA) other energy projects.
Funding:  Solar Canopy Grant Program
This program is for:  Property owners who want to install solar systems that provide multiple uses for land and water.
 

### Federal
Funding:  Commercial Energy Efficiency Tax Deduction
This program is for:  Owners or designers of new or existing commercial buildings.
Funding:  Federal Clean Energy Tax Credits
This program is for:  Businesses and for-profit organizations who installed solar energy systems, geothermal heat pumps, or small wind turbines.
Funding:  Federal Tax Credits for All-Electric and Plug-in Hybrid Vehicles
This program is for:  Businesses that use all-electric or plug-in hybrids for business purposes.
 

## Other Funding and Incentives (Miscellaneous)

Funding: MicroLoan Program

These loans currently are provided through these County partners:
Latino Economic Development Center
Life Asset

This program is for:  Nonprofit swim clubs.

Funding:  Buy Maryland Cybersecurity (BMC) Tax Credit
This program is for:  Businesses wanting to purchase cybersecurity technologies and services from a Qualified Maryland Cybersecurity Seller.
Funding:  Export MD
This program is for:  Small and mid-sized businesses wanting support with marketing costs.
Funding:  Film Production Tax Credit
This program is for:  A film production entity that incurred costs in Maryland.
Funding:  Home-based Business Exemptions
This program is for:  Small, home-based businesses.
Funding:  Manufacturing and R&D Tax Exemption
This program is for:  Businesses engaged in manufacturing and R&D.
Funding:  Maryland Commuter Tax Credit
This program is for:  Businesses, 501C3, and 501C4 organizations in Maryland.
Funding:  Small Business Relief Tax Credit
This program is for:  Small businesses that provide their employees with paid sick and safe leave.
 

### Federal
Funding:  Small Business Administration (SBA) Loans
This program is for:  All small businesses.
