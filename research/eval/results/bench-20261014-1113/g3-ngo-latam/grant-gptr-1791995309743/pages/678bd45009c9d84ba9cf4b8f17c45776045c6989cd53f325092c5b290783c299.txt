Call for WHO/IBP network SPARK grant proposals for community-based organisations worldwide that use WHO guidelines and tools
















21 September, 2026
Dear HIFA colleagues,

This is an exciting opportunity for community-based organisations worldwide that use WHO guidelines and tools.

Grants are available up to $20,000 per grant.

The emphasis on use of WHO guidelines and tools strongly aligns with HIFA's remit.

HIFA itself is not eligible, but if YOU would like to apply, please consider the possibility of including HIFA in your project plan.

How can HIFA support your project? HIFA can support your work by inclusion of a dedicated HIFA Spotlight. This would enable a focused 7-day discussion on the HIFA forums exploring your key priorities. Partnering with HIFA would raise the visibility of your work and would extend the reach of your engagement and consultation. For example, HIFA could help you tap into the experience and expertise of our 20,000 members in 180 countries. www.hifa.org/spotlights

If you would like to explore collaboration with HIFA, please contact neil@hifa.org

Best wishes, Neil

Shifting Power through Action and Reciprocal Knowledge Exchange (SPARK)

17 September 2026 Call for proposals

SPARK — Shifting Power through Action and Reciprocal Knowledge Exchange — is a grant program launched by the WHO/IBP Network designed to put communities at the centre of sexual reproductive, maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health and rights work. SPARK invests directly in youth-led, women-led, and indigenous-led community-based organizations that are reimagining how WHO guidelines and tools are disseminated, adapted, and applied in real-world settings. This is not a call for organizations to implement someone else's agenda. It is an invitation to lead. We want to learn from you: How are you using evidence-based guidance? How are you innovating? How are you shifting power to ensure communities, not institutions, drive the agenda for sexual, reproductive, maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health in your setting?

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

We are seeking proposals from youth-led, women-led, or indigenous-led community-based organizations that are:

Applying WHO guidelines and tools in innovative, locally grounded ways

Integrating sexual, reproductive, maternal, newborn, child and adolescent health into sectors beyond traditional health programming, with particular interest in initiatives at the intersection of sexual and reproductive health and rights and:

Maternal Health

Climate

Workforce / Livelihoods/Poverty reduction

Education

Faith-based initiatives

Shifting power within their programming by meaningfully involving community members in design, decision-making, and evaluation, not just delivery

Documenting and sharing innovative approaches so that lessons can strengthen the broader IBP Network and inform how WHO guidance is used at the community level and gaps that remain.

WHO CAN APPLY

We strongly encourage applications from:

Youth-led, Women-led, or Indigenous-led organizations

Community-based organisations working directly with the populations they serve

Organisations that have been operating for at least 2 years

Organisations with implementation experience using at least one WHO guideline, tool or product previously

Priority will be given to organizations that are new to WHO/IBP Network funding mechanisms and that demonstrate strong community accountability and leadership.

GRANT DETAILS

Number of grants: 5–10

Grant amount: Up to USD $20,000 per grant

Timeline: One Year

Geographic reach: We aim to fund at least one organization per WHO region (African Region, Region of the Americas, South-East Asia Region, European Region, Eastern Mediterranean Region, Western Pacific Region)

WHAT GRANTEES WILL RECEIVE

Direct, flexible grant funding to implement their proposed work

Technical support from the WHO/IBP Network and access to relevant WHO SRMNCAH guidelines and tools

Connection to IBP's global Communities of Practice for peer exchange and learning

WHO Guidelines CoP

Adolescent CoP

Women’s Health CoP

Powershifting CoP

Opportunities to document and showcase their work to a global SRMNCAH audience

PROPOSAL REQUIREMENTS

Applicants should submit an initial concept note of no more than 500 words that includes:

Organizational overview — who you are, how long you have been operating, your community base, prior implementation experience and familiarity with WHO guidelines and tools, and your leadership structure (demonstrating youth, women, and/or indigenous leadership)

Proposed activity — what you plan to do, including which WHO SRMNCAH guideline(s) or tool(s) you will use or adapt, and which sector(s) of integration you are focusing on (health, maternal health, climate, workforce, education, faith, or other)

Power-shifting approach — how your proposed work centers community voice and decision-making

Budget — a proposed budget of up to $20,000

Timeline — proposed implementation timeline and key milestones

Documentation and learning plan — how you will capture and share what you learn with your community and with the IBP Network

Shortlisted applicants may be requested to submit a more formal proposal, including financial statements, one letter of support, and WHO requirements for working with non-state actors.

HOW TO APPLY

Link to submit here. https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSfCqry5p0ONwXR7zHP9Fk2WfKqR7ZYl...

Applications may be submitted in English, French, or Spanish.

Deadline: 9 October 2026

Submit any questions to:

ibpnetwork@who.int
