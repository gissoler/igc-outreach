## Who can apply

You must be a Health Center Program award recipient with an active H80 grant award to apply.

Review the Eligibility Information in the NOFO for details.

## Key funding details

We expect to award $125 million to 357 health centers. You may apply for up to $350,000 for each year in the two-year period of performance.

You will use ENS funding to increase access to nutrition services. You must use ENS funding to increase your nutrition services patients or visits.

## How to apply

Apply on Grants.gov by 11:59 p.m. ET on September 9, 2026 .

There is no HRSA Electronic Handbooks (EHBs) application phase.
