HRSA awards $19 million in grants for telehealth services | AHA News

Skip to main content

Sep 23, 2026 - 03:16 PM

The Health Resources and Services Administration announced Sept. 22 that it will award $19 million in grants to expand various telehealth learning services. The funds will be awarded to programs for testing telehealth solutions to expand access to care and improve outcomes, increasing access to nutrition services for preventing and managing chronic diseases, and improving provider retention and expanding access to care in rural areas using technology-enabled learning models.
