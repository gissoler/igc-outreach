##
Introduction to
telehealth for maternal health services

Find information and resources on how to use telehealth to improve maternal health care access.

Disparities in maternal health care has long been a struggle, especially for those in rural and minority communities. Telehealth is one way to bridge the gaps.
This best practice guide provides tools and resources to help health care providers effectively use telehealth to deliver maternal health care for pregnant and postpartum individuals. The information can be used to establish a new program or expand and enhance an existing telehealth program.
