### Overview

Following evaluation of the health care needs of Sumapaz&rsquo;s rural population, a comprehensive care model for rural health was created in 2001 to guarantee access to health care. The initiative promotes community engagement and empowerment to design contextualized intervention strategies and so offer solutions to real life problems and challenges.
