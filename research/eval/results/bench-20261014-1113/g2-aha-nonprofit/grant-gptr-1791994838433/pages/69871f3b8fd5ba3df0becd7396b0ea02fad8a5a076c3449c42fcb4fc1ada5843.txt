# Towards the next generation of frontier Artificial Intelligence models for healthcare
This grant supports activities directed at developing and enabling the next generation of frontier Artificial Intelligence (AI) models for healthcare, contributing to the strategic sector of life sciences and healthcare in Europe. It aims to foster the full integration of advanced AI in the health sector by combining multimodal health data and leveraging high-performance computing facilities such as AI Factories, in alignment with European regulations like the forthcoming European Health Data Space (EHDS) and the AI Act.

Coverage Eligible costs will take the form of a lump sum as defined in the Horizon Europe Programme – the Framework Programme for Research and Innovation (2021-2027).

Grant application conditions Eligible Countries Open to legal entities established in Member States and Associated Countries as described in Annex B of the Horizon Europe Work Programme General Annexes. Non-eligible and non-associated countries may be able to participate under certain provisions.
Participation Restriction Participation is limited to legal entities established in Member States and Associated Countries. Entities controlled directly or indirectly by a non-eligible country or by a non-eligible country entity may not participate unless guarantees are provided and positively assessed. Entities controlled by China or Chinese legal entities cannot submit such guarantees.
Consortium Requirements Proposals should ensure multidisciplinary approaches and a broad representation of stakeholders including healthcare professionals, patients, biomedical scientists, AI developers, data engineers, and ethics experts.
Admissibility and Proposal Format Proposals must follow the requirements in Annex A and E of the Horizon Europe Work Programme General Annexes, and respect the page limits and layout described in the application form.
Use of EU Data-Infrastructure If projects use satellite-based earth observation, positioning, navigation, and/or timing data and services, beneficiaries must make use of Copernicus and/or Galileo/EGNOS. Other data and services may additionally be used.
Financial and Operational Capacity Applicants must show financial and operational capacity as described in Annex C of the Work Programme General Annexes.
Lump Sum Grant Eligible costs will be reimbursed as a lump sum. Details are available in the Horizon Europe decision on lump sum contributions.
Documentation https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/HORIZON-HLTH-2027-03-TOOL-08

Eligible expenses Eligible expenses are those necessary for executing the Coordination and Support Actions (CSA) in accordance with the objectives described above. Detailed eligible costs are described in the legal and financial setup (Annex G of the Horizon Europe Work Programme General Annexes).

Evaluation criteria Excellence: Minimum threshold 4/5

Impact: Minimum threshold 4/5

Implementation: Minimum threshold 4/5

Cumulative threshold: 12/15. See Annex D of the Horizon Europe Work Programme General Annexes for the full award criteria.

Business exclusions Legal entities established outside eligible Member States and Associated Countries; entities controlled (directly or indirectly) by non-eligible countries/entities unless guarantees are accepted (exclusion for Chinese ownership/control is absolute).

Entities unable to demonstrate financial and operational capacity as required by the programme.

Reporting Reporting requirements follow Horizon Europe Coordination and Support Actions standards and are described in the Annotated Model Grant Agreement and the Application Guidelines.

Application process Applicants must submit their proposal using the standard application form (HE CSA) through the Funding & Tenders Portal Submission System before the deadline.

Application documents must comply with admissibility, eligibility, and proposal format rules as described in the Work Programme and as set out in the submission system.

Application requirements Completed standard application form (HE CSA)

Budget breakdown and cost justification following lump sum guidelines
