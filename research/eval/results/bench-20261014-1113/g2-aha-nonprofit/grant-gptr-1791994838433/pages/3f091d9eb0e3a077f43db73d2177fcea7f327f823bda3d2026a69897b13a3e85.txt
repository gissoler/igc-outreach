### Call for proposals to support factory-level de-manufacturing and re-manufacturing technologies

October 14, 2026; Horizon Europe funding opportunities ; AI Grants , Digital technologies grants , Innovation grants , Sustainability funding

Call for proposals to support projects developing de-manufacturing and re-manufacturing technologies at the factory level aims to enhance both circularity and resilience across manufacturing industries. Selected initiatives will receive grant awards ranging between EUR 5.00 million and 6.50 million, drawing from a total available funding pool of EUR 36.00 million. Proposals must focus on advance solutions such as AI-driven disassembly, predictive maintenance analysis, digital twin integration, or on-site component repair. To be eligible, applicants must participate as part of a consortium that includes at least three independent legal entities from distinct Member States or Associated Countries.

### Call for proposals to support the development of innovative AI foundation models for the energy sector

### Call for proposals to support the advancement of trustworthy, human-centric cognitive AI

July 23, 2026; European Innovation Council (EIC) grants ; AI Grants , Digital technologies grants , Innovation grants

Call for proposals to support projects that aim to move beyond the current state-of-the-art in traditional AI approaches focuses on significantly improving the reasoning, abstraction, and planning capabilities of artificial intelligence systems. Open to legal entities forming eligible consortia of at least three independent partners from different Member States or Associated Countries, the initiative seeks to overcome the limitations of standard deep learning and symbolic frameworks. ### Call for proposals to support the development of an AI Foundation Toxicology Model and standardized weight-of-evidence framework

July 06, 2026; Horizon Europe funding opportunities , Innovative Health Initiative (IHI) Grants ; AI Grants , Health Grants , Innovation grants , Research grants

Call for proposals to develop a transparent, AI-driven Foundation Toxicology Model and a standardized weight-of-evidence framework. This initiative aims to address and standardize the regulatory waiver process for second-species chronic and sub-chronic small-molecule general toxicology studies, thereby reducing reliance on animal testing, lowering costs, and accelerating medicine development timelines without compromising patient safety. Eligibility is open to any legal entity globally, provided they meet Horizon Europe conditions, though consortia must include at least one independent entity from an EU Member State and two others from different Member States or Associated Countries. Selected projects are expected to consolidate diverse pharmaceutical data sources into a secure new database, select and optimize interpretable AI methods, and establish an early regulatory strategy to secure formal model endorsement from authorities like the EMA and FDA.

### Call for proposals to support the development of innovations related to Quantum Machine Learning

July 06, 2026; Horizon Europe funding opportunities ; AI Grants , Digital technologies grants , Innovation grants

Call for proposals to support projects that advance multiple key research directions in Quantum Machine Learning (QML), balancing scientific excellence with industrial application. The initiative aims to develop, validate, and integrate scalable, quantum-enhanced AI approaches—such as Quantum Supervised Learning, Quantum Convolutional Neural Networks, and Quantum Reinforcement Learning—into existing pipelines to address massive data volumes and complex computational tasks. Eligibility is open to any legal entity globally, provided they meet Horizon Europe conditions and form a consortium consisting of at least one independent entity from an EU Member State and two from different Member States or Associated Countries. Ultimately, the selected projects must provide clear IP plans under the European Union Public Licence (EUPL-1.2) and deliver robust, noise-aware QML models that demonstrate measurable performance gains over classical baselines in real-world domains like climate modeling, healthcare, and cybersecurity.

### Call for applications to support pre-seed and seed-stage AI startups with funding, mentorship, and resources to foster innovation and establish sustainable business models

June 24, 2026; Non-EU / Global / International Grants ; AI Grants , Business development / Entrepreneurship , Startup grants

Call for applications for a program designed to spearhead the development, commercialisation, and business model refinement of early-stage AI startups. The initiative aims to build a full-cycle platform that positions Italy as a primary hub in the global AI market by fostering a diverse network of cutting-edge businesses with transformative potential. The program target startups in the pre-seed and seed stages that have reached the MVP, prototype, or initial market traction phase. ### Call for applications to support AI-driven projects advancing science and safety

June 04, 2026; Non-EU / Global / International Grants ; AI Grants , Digital technologies grants

This initiative is open to individuals, teams, and organizations—both non-profit and for-profit—and offers grant funding ranging from USD 10,000 to USD 100,000. Support is distributed across eight distinct focus areas, including AI for security, decentralized and cooperative AI, longevity biotechnology, molecular nanotechnology, and brain-computer interfaces. Projects will be prioritized based on their alignment with these focus areas, their feasibility within short artificial general intelligence (AGI) timelines, their open-source preference, and their potential to reduce existential risks. Rather than just providing financial backing, the program offers a comprehensive ecosystem that includes zero-cost office space and local, private compute resources. To foster peer collaboration and community, the Foresight Institute strongly prioritizes “AI-first” applicants who intend to be active, in-person members at one of their designated hubs in Berlin or San Francisco. Grant recipients are also invited to attend travel-paid workshops and collaborative sprints designed to advance the frontiers of their respective fields. Consequently, applications requesting funding only, without hub participation, will be considered solely in exceptional circumstances.

### Call for proposals to support doctoral candidates in developing innovative AI systems for scientific advancement

June 03, 2026; Horizon Europe funding opportunities ; AI Grants , Education grants , Innovation grants , Research grants

Call for proposals to fund doctoral candidates and Doctoral Networks dedicated to developing or significantly advancing innovative AI systems, models, tools, or methodologies within specific scientific domains. Successful applicants will be integrated into RAISE (Resource for AI Science in Europe), becoming active participants in its collaborative research community. Rather than focusing strictly on core computer science or using existing AI software for standard data processing, projects must demonstrate a direct dependence on novel AI algorithms to drive advanced scientific inquiry and push the boundaries of the chosen discipline. A total funding pool of EUR 30,000,000 is available for this initiative. Expected Outcomes For Doctoral Candidates: Participants will receive specialized doctoral-level training on AI in science. The program aims to equip researchers with advanced transferable skills, expand their professional networks, and enhance their employability both within and outside of academia. For Participating Organizations: The call aims to improve the quality and sustainability of doctoral training programs, enhance cross-sectoral knowledge transfer, increase research and innovation capacity, and accelerate the uptake of AI technologies across diverse scientific disciplines.

### EIC Tech to Market Entrepreneurship

January 08, 2024; European Innovation Council (EIC) grants ; AI Grants , Biotechnology grants , Business development / Entrepreneurship , DeepTech

The EIC Tech to Market (T2M) Programme is designed to help researchers and innovators who have received funding from the EIC Pathfinder and Transition programs to bring their deep-tech projects from the laboratory to the marketplace. It offers a range of services and support, including customized training to build entrepreneurial skills, expert guidance on developing and validating business models, and assistance in creating strong value propositions. The program is divided into two main components: an Entrepreneurship Programme focused on training and skill development, and a Venture Building Programme that provides hands-on support for creating new startups. Through a combination of bootcamps, workshops, coaching, and networking opportunities, the T2M Programme aims to accelerate the journey of deep-tech innovations to market success.
