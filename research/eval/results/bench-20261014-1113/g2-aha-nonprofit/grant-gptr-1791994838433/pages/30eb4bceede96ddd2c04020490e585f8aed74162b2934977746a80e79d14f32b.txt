## Introduction
This project aims to harmonize a list of COVID-19 data elements with several Common Data Models (CDMs) and open standards. These data elements have been identified by the COVID-19 Evidence Accelerator Collaborative initiative led by Reagan-Udall Foundation , FDA and Friends of Cancer Research .
