Call for proposals to support the advancement of trustworthy, human-centric cognitive AI - EU Funding

Skip to content

Call for proposals to support projects that aim to move beyond the current state-of-the-art in traditional AI approaches focuses on significantly improving the reasoning, abstraction, and planning capabilities of artificial intelligence systems. Open to legal entities forming eligible consortia of at least three independent partners from different Member States or Associated Countries, the initiative seeks to overcome the limitations of standard deep learning and symbolic frameworks. Proposals must explore novel, interdisciplinary approaches—such as neuro-symbolic AI—to handle multimodal data, ensure provable trustworthiness and alignment with the EU AI Act, and demonstrate integrated cognitive AI systems at Technology Readiness Level 4.

## About EU Funding Portal
EUFundingPortal.eu is an independent, searchable database of exciting grants and funding opportunities, currently featuring 335 calls for proposals from the European Union and other international sources .

We help small businesses, non-profit organizations, research organizations, and universities find exciting grants and funding opportunities from both the European Union and around the world.
