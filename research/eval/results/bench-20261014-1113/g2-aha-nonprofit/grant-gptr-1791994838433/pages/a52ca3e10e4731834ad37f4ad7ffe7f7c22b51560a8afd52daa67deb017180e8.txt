# GenAI4EU: Funding opportunities to boost Generative AI “made in Europe”

Several EU funding opportunities and projects are integrating generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) in Europe’s strategic sectors, and keep their competitive edge.

Researchers, innovators, companies and other interested organisations can find EU funding opportunities to join forces to develop and deploy generative AI in Europe’s strategic sectors as part of the GenAI4EU flagship initiative . The initiative is now surpassing the initial commitment of €500 million announced in the AI innovation package in January 2024, with close to €700 million funding planned in Horizon Europe , the Digital Europe Programme , and the European Innovation Council (EIC) .

Three pilot projects for GenAI for public administration were already launched. They aim to accelerate the responsible adoption of AI across strategic sectors, including the public sector. These projects bring together public administrations, research organisations and technology providers from across Europe to demonstrate how GenAI can streamline decision-making, internal processes, accessibility, and bureaucratic procedures.

Explore the calls for proposals supporting the development and uptake of generative AI under the GenAI4EU initiative.

Other calls can also be found on the EU’s Funding & Tenders Portal . Stakeholders are encouraged to use the b2match platform for networking and matchmaking. Support in the application process is offered by Ideal-Ist .

### GenAI4EU: moving generative AI from a promising potential to concrete applications

Researchers, innovators, industrial companies and others applying to these EU funding opportunities will become part of GenAI4EU. It is the Commission’s flagship initiative to support the development and deployment of generative AI solutions across Europe’s industrial ecosystems and the public sector, including public administrations.

The specific sectors – manufacturing, robotics, health, energy, agrifood, mobility, and aerospace – are aligned with those identified in the EU Industrial Strategy and the Draghi Report . In parallel, GenAI4EU also supports the uptake of generative AI in the public sector, including public administrations.

GenAI4EU will support the transition pathways outlined in these strategies by driving innovation, streamlining production, and fostering sustainable growth in these sectors through the tailored adoption of generative AI. This initiative will promote the development of large, open innovation ecosystems that encourage collaboration between AI developers—including startups and scale-ups—and strategic European industrial players and public sector actors.

As such, GenAI4EU contributes to the Commission’s overarching goals of enhancing competitiveness and economic growth through AI, as outlined in the  AI Continent Action Plan  and the Apply AI strategy . GenAI4EU aims to increase the adoption of AI among EU companies , which currently stands at just 13.5%. This highlights the significant potential for growth, innovation, and competitiveness through broader AI uptake.

This initiative builds on the Horizon Europe ’s AI Boost project (2023) , which awarded €1 million and 8 million GPU hours to four winners developing large AI models. It also builds on the Digital Europe Programme (DEP) support for open-source, multilingual large language models through OpenEuroLLM . Ongoing efforts aim to embed generative AI in future calls across Horizon Europe, DEP , and the EIC for 2026 and 2027, reinforcing Europe’s position as a global hub for secure, inclusive, and innovative AI technologies.
