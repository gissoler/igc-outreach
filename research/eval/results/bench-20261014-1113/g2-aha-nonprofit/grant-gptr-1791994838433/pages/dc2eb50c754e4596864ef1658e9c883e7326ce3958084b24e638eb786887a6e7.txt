# Capacity Building in International Dementia Research Program (CBIDR)

The Alzheimer&#x27;s Association&#x27;s CBIDR program funds institutions in low- and middle-income countries to build sustainable infrastructure for dementia research and capacity development.
Overview

Funder: Alzheimer&#x27;s Association

Due Dates: June 3, 2026 (Letter of Intent) | September 2, 2026 (Full application)

Funding Amounts: Up to $250,000/year (direct + indirect costs); total not to exceed $1,000,000 over 3–5 years.

Summary: Supports institutions in low- and middle-income countries to build sustainable dementia research infrastructure and capacity.

Key Information: Traditional research studies and major equipment purchases are not eligible; focus is on institutional capacity building.

## Description

This program, offered by the Alzheimer&#x27;s Association in partnership with the Global Brain Health Institute at UCSF, aims to strengthen the research infrastructure of institutions located in low- and middle-income countries (LMICs). The focus is on overcoming barriers to sustainable scientific investigation in dementia by funding initiatives that build institutional capacity—such as developing training programs, creating culturally relevant research tools, collecting normative data, enhancing community outreach, building research databases, and supporting essential research personnel. The program does not fund traditional research studies or major equipment purchases (unit price over $10,000), but instead supports activities that enable institutions to expand and sustain their research portfolios in the field of dementia.
