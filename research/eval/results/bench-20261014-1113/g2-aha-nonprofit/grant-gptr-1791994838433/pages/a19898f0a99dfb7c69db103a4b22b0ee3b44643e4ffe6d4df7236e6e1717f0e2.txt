## About This Grant
The National Institute on Aging&#x27;s Artificial Intelligence and Technology Collaboratories (AITC) program promotes the development and implementation of AI approaches and technology for aging and Alzheimer&#x27;s Disease and related dementias (ADRD) research.
The program supports P30 Center Core awards that serve as collaborative hubs bringing together AI/ML researchers, clinicians, and aging scientists to develop AI tools for cognitive impairment identification, prediction of disease trajectories, and optimization of care delivery for older adults. A companion U24 Coordinating Center award supports cross-site coordination, shared infrastructure, and standardized evaluation frameworks.
NIA committed $21. 5 million total in FY 2026 across both the P30 (RFA-AG-26-006) and U24 (RFA-AG-26-007) mechanisms. While the current application cycle has closed, the AITC continues as an active multi-year program with potential for future funding opportunities.
