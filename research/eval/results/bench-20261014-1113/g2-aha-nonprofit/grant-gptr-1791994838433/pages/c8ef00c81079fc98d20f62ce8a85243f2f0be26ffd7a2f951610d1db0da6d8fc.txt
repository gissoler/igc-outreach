## Featured Sessions and Events:
Emerging Data, Collective Impact: A Global Consortium Approach to Alzheimer’s Research
July 15, 12:30-1:45 PM BST | Crown Plaza London Docklands
Global efforts in Alzheimer's disease and related dementias are increasingly converging through collaborative consortia that integrate diverse datasets, technologies, and expertise to accelerate early scientific insights. This symposium will feature speakers from ADNI, AMYPAD, GNPC, and WW-FINGERS to explore how these intersecting consortium models are reshaping the research landscape and expanding opportunities for open, global discovery.
Poster: The Alzheimer’s Disease Data Initiative: Enabling Research-ready, Multi-modal Data for Dementia Research
July 14 | Exhibition Hall
View the AD Data Initiative Research Team’s poster about AD Workbench, a free, secure, cloud-based research environment for Alzheimer’s and related dementias scientists.
