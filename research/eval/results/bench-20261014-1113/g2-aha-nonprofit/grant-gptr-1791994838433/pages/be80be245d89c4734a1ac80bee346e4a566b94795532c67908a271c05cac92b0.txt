# Alzheimer's Disease and Related Dementias Funding Opportunities

Below is a list of current NIA NOFOs in Alzheimer's disease and related dementias research, in order of expiration date. Click on the title to go to the published NOFO.

In the interest of increasing speed and efficiency for issuing AD/ADRD NOFOs, NIA has created two “parent” NOFOs—PAR-25-332 for R01s and PAR-25-331 for R21s. Over the three years that these are open, NIA will issue other related notices describing high-priority research topics that fall within the purview of the parents. You’ll see the relevant notices below.
